1. Who we are

LDBG is a falling-block RPG played in a web browser at play.ldbg.net, with an optional Windows app. It is made and run by DjYale, an individual developer in Connecticut, USA ("we", "us"). In these terms, "the game" means the game, its website at ldbg.net, the Windows app and the servers behind them.

Purchases are sold to you by Tebex Limited, not by us (see Buying things).

2. Agreeing to these terms

By logging in or playing, you agree to these terms and to our Privacy Policy. If you don't agree, please don't play. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian should read these with you.

When you log in and haven't yet agreed to the current version of these terms, the game shows a card saying "By continuing you agree to LDBG's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy", with links to both, and two buttons: AGREE and LOG OUT. When you press AGREE, our server records which version of these terms you agreed to, and when. Until you have, you can't buy anything. When we change these terms in a way you need to agree to, the card comes back once, the next time you log in, and never in the middle of a run. If you press LOG OUT instead, nothing is recorded, and you can still play the guest demo, which keeps no account.

3. Age

You must be 13 or older to play LDBG. If you're under 18, you need a parent's or guardian's permission to play, and to buy anything. If we learn that a player is under 13, we delete their account. A parent who thinks their child under 13 has an account can write to support@ldbg.net and we will delete it.

4. An early beta

LDBG is in early beta. That means:

  • It may be slow, broken or unavailable at times, and some things will change as we work on it.
  • We may rebalance the game: change what items do, what enemies drop, what things cost, and how hard things are.
  • We don't plan to wipe accounts. But in an emergency (a serious bug, an exploit, or lost data) we may have to undo, reset or remove progress, items or Gold, without paying anything back. We'll keep that as small as we can.
  • Things you bought are never wiped. If we ever have to reset progress, purchases stay on your account.

5. Your account

  • You log in through a sign-in service run by Clockwork Labs (SpacetimeAuth), with Google, GitHub, Discord or an email login. Keep that login safe: whoever has it has your account.
  • One person, one account to play from at a time. Don't share your account, and don't play someone else's.
  • The account is yours to use, not to sell, give away or trade.
  • You can play as a guest without an account. A guest game is a demo: nothing in it is kept, and it never moves to an account.
  • Restricted accounts are a way to play where you make or find everything yourself. You choose it before your first run, and you can leave it but never go back. A restricted account can't use the Player Market or risk duels, and takes nothing from other players in the Dark modes.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the account menu (see Ending your account).

Items, Gold, levels, skins, records and everything else you earn or buy in LDBG are part of the game, not property you own. Your licence lets you use them in the game.

8. Losing things to other players

Some parts of LDBG put your gear at stake, and you can lose it to other players. This is part of the game.

  • Risk duels in the Arena: the winner takes every item the loser is wearing.
  • The Dark modes: a Dark duel can cost you your worn gear and what you carried, a Dark Farm round takes part of your harvest, and a Dark death can break your gear.
  • Leaving counts as losing. Closing the game, losing your connection, or logging in somewhere else during a duel counts as a loss, and you lose what was at stake.

The game tells you what's at stake before you enter. We don't give back things lost this way. Things you bought are never at stake: they can't be worn into a risk, lost or taken.

9. No real-money trading

You may not buy, sell or trade accounts, items, Gold or anything else from the game for real money or anything outside the game, or offer to. That includes using the Player Market to pass value to someone who paid you somewhere else, or to move value between your own accounts.

If we find it, we may undo the trades, remove the items or Gold, and suspend or close the accounts involved.

10. Fair play

Play the game yourself. You may not:

  • use bots, macros, scripts or anything else that plays for you;
  • use a modified client, or tools that read or change the game while it runs;
  • use a bug or exploit for gain. If you find one, tell us at support@ldbg.net;
  • play on more than one account to help yourself, or arrange duels or matches with someone to fix the result.

The game checks runs on its servers, and we look at play statistics to spot bots. A person looks at what the checks find and decides; nothing bans you automatically.

11. How to behave

  • Pick a name you'd be fine with anyone seeing. No slurs, nothing hateful or sexual, and nothing pretending to be us or another player. Names that break this are changed.
  • In chat, and anywhere else you can write: no harassment, threats, hate, sexual content, spam, scams or advertising, and nobody's personal information.
  • Don't ask anyone for their login or password, and don't share your own.
  • Nothing illegal, and nothing that puts a young player at risk.
  • Don't harass other players outside the game because of it, either.

You can block a player and report a name or a chat line from the game. You can also write to support@ldbg.net.

12. What we can do about it

If you break these terms, or we reasonably believe you have, we may:

  • change your display name;
  • delete your chat lines, or mute you in chat for a time;
  • undo trades or results, and remove items or Gold gained by breaking the rules;
  • suspend your account, for a time or for good;
  • close your account.

We choose what fits what happened, and we tell you what we did and why, in the game where we can, or by email. If you think we got it wrong, write to support@ldbg.net and a person will look at it again. Deleting your account doesn't end a suspension or a mute.

Things you bought aren't refunded if your account is suspended or closed for breaking these terms, except where the law says otherwise.

13. Buying things

LDBG is free to play. Some looks can be bought with real money on the Store's PREMIUM page.

Who sells them

Purchases are sold by Tebex Limited, a company in England and Wales, as the seller (the "merchant of record"). Tebex takes your payment, charges any tax, sends your receipt and handles refunds. Tebex's own terms apply to the payment, alongside these. You pay on Tebex's page, and we never see your card or payment details.

What you get

  • What you buy is a look: today, an ornament kit that changes how one piece of gear looks. It gives no power, no advantage and nothing random. You see exactly what you're buying before you pay.
  • It is a licence to use that look in the game on your account, like any other item (see Items, Gold and progress). It has no cash value.
  • It is bound to your account: it can't be traded, listed on the Market, sold to a shop, staked, lost in a duel or the Dark modes, or given to anyone. If the piece of gear it's on is ever lost, the kit comes back to you.
  • It arrives in your bag once Tebex tells our server the payment went through. That's usually at once; if it's slow, our server checks with Tebex every day.

Prices and limits

  • Prices are shown in US dollars before you buy. Any tax is added on Tebex's page before you pay.
  • There is one confirmation, no timers and no "only a few left". Nothing is pre-ticked.
  • Each account can spend at most $100 a month.
  • You must have an account to buy; guests can't. Restricted accounts can buy looks too.

14. Refunds

Because what you buy is delivered at once, purchases are final, except that we will refund:

  • an accidental purchase, if you ask within 14 days and haven't used it;
  • a purchase made by a player under 18 without a parent's or guardian's permission;
  • something you paid for that never arrived;
  • a billing mistake, such as being charged twice;
  • and wherever the law gives you a right to one. Nothing here takes away a right you have under the law where you live.

In the EU and the UK, you normally have 14 days to cancel a purchase of digital content. Tebex's checkout asks you to agree that the look is delivered at once and that you lose that right once it is. If you didn't agree to that, the 14 days still apply.

To ask for a refund, write to support@ldbg.net from your account (the game shows your purchases under the PREMIUM page), or contact Tebex's support. Tebex pays the refund back to how you paid.

When a purchase is refunded, what it gave you is taken back, and nothing else. If an ornament kit is on a piece of gear, the look comes off and the gear stays yours, plain.

15. Chargebacks

If you dispute a payment with your bank or card company instead of asking us or Tebex for a refund:

  • what that purchase gave you is taken back while the dispute is open;
  • your account can't buy anything until it's settled;
  • if the dispute ends in our favour, you get the item back and can buy again;
  • if it ends in yours, purchases stay paused until you've sorted it out with us at support@ldbg.net.

A disputed payment never costs you the rest of your account: your progress, Gold, other items and other purchases stay as they are. Please ask us first; it's quicker.
