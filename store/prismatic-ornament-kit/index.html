Prismatic Ornament Kit

Gives one piece of gear a shifting rainbow finish. Looks only, and you can take it off again.

$4.99 USD

Any tax is added on the checkout page, before you pay.

Buy up to 5 at a time, one for each piece you want to dress.

Buy in the game

Open LDBG, log in, then go to STORE › PREMIUM. There is no checkout on this site.

What you get

  • One Prismatic Ornament Kit. It gives one piece of gear the Prismatic finish. The piece's own shading drifts through the colours of the rainbow, slowly and all the time, in your bag and on your character.
  • Looks only. It changes how the piece looks and nothing else: no stats, no effect, nothing in a fight, a drop or a price.
  • Other players see it on your character: in duels, in co-op, and wherever your character is shown.
  • You can take it off at any time, for free, and the kit goes back to your bag to put on another piece.
  • One kit gives one piece its finish at a time. To give the finish to more than one piece at once, buy more than one kit.

How it's delivered

  • You pay on Tebex's page, in its own window. As soon as Tebex tells the game's server the payment went through, the kit is put straight into your in-game bag, and the game tells you it has arrived. That's usually at once. If it's slow, the server also checks with Tebex every day.
  • No code to type and nothing to download.
  • It's bound to your account: it can never be traded, listed on the Market, sold to a shop, salvaged, staked in a duel or lost in the Dark modes.
  • If the piece wearing it is ever lost or taken, the kit comes back to your bag. While a piece wears a kit, take the kit off before you sell, list or salvage that piece.

How to put it on

  1. Open INVENTORY.
  2. Choose a piece of gear, in your bag or one you're wearing.
  3. On its sheet, press ORNAMENT, then pick the Prismatic Ornament Kit.

To take it off, press REMOVE KIT on the piece's sheet. Food and materials can't wear a kit.

Who can buy it

  • Anyone logged in to an LDBG account. The guest demo can't buy anything.
  • You must be 13 or older. If you're under 18, ask a parent or guardian first.
  • Restricted accounts can buy it too: it's looks, not something to make or find.
  • Each account can spend at most $100 a month in the store.

Paying, and refunds

  • $4.99 USD. It's sold to you by Tebex Limited, which takes the payment, adds any tax and sends your receipt. We never see your card.
  • Purchases are final, except an accidental purchase you report within 14 days and haven't used, one made by a player under 18 without permission, one that never arrived, a billing mistake, and wherever the law gives you more. The full refund policy.
