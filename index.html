Chapter I · The Farm

Every line is a harvest

  1. Maple Log ×3
  2. Mithril Ore ×1
  3. Raw Trout ×2

The Farm is an endless run. Clear lines and you gather logs, ore, fish and fibre from the land around you, then carry it home to craft with. The better you stack, the more you bring back: quads, T-spins and combos all pay extra.

Farm Solo

Just you and the board. Garbage rises every few pieces, so keep clearing.

Farm Versus

Up to ten players in a room, sending garbage at each other. You keep everything you gather.

Dark Farm

Versus with stakes. Half of what you gather is on the line, and the winner takes it.

Greenshore's story runs 19 fights long, from zombies on the shore to the Cult Leader at the top of the tower. Your lines are your attacks. Theirs land on your board: arrows knock out blocks, fire spreads along the bottom row, and slime costs you a rotation.

    Chapter VII · Questions

    What adventurers ask

    What is LDBG?
    LDBG is a free falling-block RPG you play in your browser. Every line you clear is an attack on the monster in front of you. You gather materials on the Farm, craft gear, fight up the Greenshore dungeons, and duel other players, and your account keeps your levels, gear and loot.
    Is LDBG free to play?
    Yes. LDBG is free and plays in your browser at redredret.github.io/ldbg. Try it as a guest, or Log In so everything you earn is kept.
    Do I need to download anything?
    No. It runs in a desktop browser such as Chrome, Edge or Firefox. There is also an optional Windows app that opens the same game in its own window, with extra settings like higher frame rates.
    What do I play it with?
    A keyboard or a controller, on a computer. Every control can be rebound in Settings, and handling such as auto-repeat and soft drop speed can be tuned the way competitive stackers like it.
    How does the RPG side work?
    Clearing lines gathers logs, ore, fish and fibre on the Farm, or deals damage in a dungeon. Bigger clears hit harder: Quads, T-spins and combos all pay extra. You earn XP and levels, spend points across 3 upgrade trees, craft and wear gear, finish quests and daily tasks, and open reward chests.
    Can I play with friends?
    Yes. Farm Versus seats up to ten players who send garbage at each other, Dark Farm puts half your haul on the line, Endless and the Slime King can be fought with a friend, and the Arena lets you duel another player for the win or for each other's gear.
    Is my progress saved?
    When you Log In, your progress is kept on the game's servers and follows you to any computer. Guest play is a short local demo, and nothing earned as a guest moves to an account.
    Is LDBG finished?
    No, it is in early beta and grows every week. New dungeons, bosses, gear and modes land often; the Recently Added list on this page shows the latest.

    Recently Added

    • 19 OctThe Endless Dungeon Endless is one dungeon now, made of regions: Greenshore Fields, Bandit Camp, Bandit Town, the Cyclops Arena and the Cultist Tower, plus the Dump once you beat the Garbage Man's third wave and the Slime Grotto once you reach wave 10 of the Slime King's pit. Each region ends in a chest, harder regions hold better chests (the Cultist Tower never pays less than a Masterwork Vault), and the hardest can pay an Arcane Vault or a Royal Chest. The deeper you go, the harder the regions get. The Garbage Man and the Slime King appear as gentler versions of themselves, and deep enemies hit less hard on a messy board. Co-op works too: regions either of you has opened can come up.
    • 14 OctBosses make an entrance Every boss now arrives in style. The Bandit King spins down out of the sky and bounces onto his spot, the Slime King lands with a splat, the Cult Leader whirls out of a burst of dark magic, and the Garbage Man hops in with a bag balanced on his head. Shooty juggles his way on, until the last stone comes down on his head, and Punchy, Bernie and the Cyclops each have a move of their own. The fight starts exactly when it always did.
    • 13 OctGel Packs A new block skin for anyone who maxes out a Slime King run, alone or with a friend: keep going until the game tells you the run has reached its limit. Pouches of coloured gel that squash flat when they land, bulge, and wobble back into place. Slam a piece down and the blocks underneath feel it too, and a line clear lands everything above it with a squish. Fights against him, and every fight, also run lighter: the health bars cost far less to draw while they move.
    • 13 OctOmens, and the Golden Slime King Now and then a Slime King walks in golden: beat him for three times his loot and up to 200 coins. And Endless and Dark have Omens. Open 30 chests in Endless or Dark to claim the Omen Seeker feat, then switch Omens on from the Endless and Dark cards. After every third chest an omen takes hold for a few kills: a boon like Wrath, Bounty or Gold Rush, a curse like Frenzy or Thick Hide, or a pact that makes the fight harder and pays far more. Every run with Omens on earns 20% more XP.
