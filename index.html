Chapter I · The Farm

Clear lines, gather materials

  1. Maple Log ×3
  2. Mithril Ore ×1

The Farm is an endless run. Clear lines to gather logs, ore, fish and fibre, then craft with them. Quads, T-spins and combos gather more.

Farm Solo

Just you and the board. Garbage rises every few pieces, so keep clearing.

Farm Versus

Up to ten players in a room, sending garbage at each other. You keep everything you gather.

Dark Farm

Versus with stakes. Half of what you gather is on the line, and the winner takes it.

Greenshore's story runs 19 fights long, from zombies on the shore to the Cult Leader at the top of the tower. Beat him and the Red Desert opens: 20 more fights across 6 places, from the Dust Bowl to the cult's dig at the Research Station, and through the gate they guard to the Unbound in the Hellish Desert. Your lines are your attacks. Theirs land on your board: arrows knock out blocks, fire spreads along the bottom row, a worm chomps your tower and a mummy binds your HOLD shut.

Chapter VII · Questions

What adventurers ask

What is LDBG?
LDBG is a free falling-block RPG you play in your browser. Every line you clear is an attack on the monster in front of you. You gather materials on the Farm, craft gear, fight through two Stories, Greenshore and the Red Desert, push on into the Endless Dungeon, and duel other players, and your account keeps your levels, gear and loot.
Is LDBG free to play?
Yes. LDBG is free and plays in your browser at play.ldbg.net. Try it as a guest, or Log In so everything you earn is kept.
Do I need to download anything?
No. It runs in a desktop browser such as Chrome, Edge or Firefox. There is also an optional Windows app that opens the same game in its own window, with extra settings like higher frame rates; it isn't signed yet, so Windows asks you to confirm before it runs.
What do I play it with?
A keyboard or a controller, on a computer. Every control can be rebound in Settings, and handling such as auto-repeat and soft drop speed can be tuned the way competitive stackers like it.
Can I play on my phone?
Not yet. LDBG needs a keyboard or a controller, so play it on a computer. Touch controls are planned.
How does the RPG side work?
Clearing lines gathers logs, ore, fish and fibre on the Farm, or deals damage in a dungeon. Bigger clears hit harder: Quads, T-spins and combos all pay extra. You earn XP and levels, spend points across 3 upgrade trees, craft gear and improve it at the Forge, finish quests, daily tasks and bounties, find pets, and open reward chests.
Can I play with friends?
Yes. Farm Versus seats up to ten players who send garbage at each other, Dark Farm puts half your haul on the line, the Endless Dungeon and the Slime King can be fought with a friend, and the Arena lets you duel another player for the win or for each other's gear. Add your friends in the game to see when they are on and invite them straight in.
Is my progress saved?
Yes. When you Log In, your progress is kept on the game's servers and follows you to any computer, and it stays through the beta: nothing you earn now will be wiped. Guest play is a short local demo, and nothing earned as a guest moves to an account.
Is LDBG finished?
No, it is in early beta and grows every week. New dungeons, bosses, gear and modes land often; the Recently Added list on this page shows the latest.

Recently Added

  • 18 NovFairer duels Thorns no longer win fights on their own: in a duel, Thorned and the Ring of Thorns hand back a small share that adds up over the fight. Heals were made for monsters, so in a duel Mending heals an eighth, and Vampiric, the Hourglass Amulet and the Combat tree's heals count at half. Dark now looks for an opponent within ten levels, in your own region first, then your part of the world, then anywhere.
  • 18 NovThe Red Desert's Story, told in full The Red Desert's Story has a new opening and a new ending. Vesper joins Katherine and Brann on the road in, and once the Unbound falls and the portal goes quiet, stay to hear what became of the Old Man, and what the cultists are really after. You get a few words of your own, too.
  • 17 NovRook's hunts and diaries reach the end of the desert Rook can now send you after Sand Veil Cultists in the Research Station, and those kills count toward Greenshore's Cultists too, or after the Unbound himself in the Hellish Desert. The Research Station and the Hellish Desert have diaries of their own, Easy to Elite, with a badge each; their diaries pay Blank Charts. And the Desert Orb now needs a fifth soul: the Lesser Cult Leader's, along with the four desert bosses. The Unbound's soul is not wanted.
  • 17 NovDesert Master, and the desert cult's idols A new Feat, Desert Master: kill the Unbound ten times in the Dark Endless Dungeon, and every cultist takes 10% more damage from you, the Sand Veil and their leader as well as Greenshore's. With Greenshore Master too, Greenshore's cultists take 20% more. And the Sand Veil Cultist and the Lesser Cult Leader can now drop the Cult Idol, as Greenshore's cultists do. Runs you started before this keep the old rules.
