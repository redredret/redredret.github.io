I

Chapter I · The Farm

Every line is a harvest

  1. Maple Log ×3
  2. Mithril Ore ×1
  3. Raw Trout ×2
  4. Flax ×4

The Farm is an endless run. Clear lines and you gather logs, ore, fish and fibre from the land around you, then carry it home to craft with. The better you stack, the more you bring back: quads, T-spins and combos all pay extra.

Farm Solo

Just you and the board. Garbage rises every few pieces, so keep clearing.

Farm Versus

Up to ten players in a room, sending garbage at each other. You keep everything you gather.

Dark Farm

Versus with stakes. Half of what you gather is on the line, and the winner takes it.

II

Chapter II · The Armoury

From leather to adamantite

  1. Lv 1
  2. Lv 10
  3. Lv 30
  4. Lv 35

Smelt your ore into ingots, weave your fibre into fabric, and work them into 49 pieces of gear across 57 recipes. Everything you wear shows on your character, from helmets and armour to swords, bows, staves, bucklers, rings and necklaces. Trade the rest for Gold with other players on the Market.

    95 items. Hover or tap one to look closer.

    III

    Chapter III · Dungeons

    Fight your way up the coast

    1. The Coast
    2. Bandit Country
    3. Cyclops Arena
    4. Cultist Tower

    Greenshore's story runs 19 fights long, from zombies on the shore to the Cult Leader at the top of the tower. Your lines are your attacks. Theirs land on your board: arrows knock out blocks, fire spreads along the bottom row, and slime costs you a rotation.

    • StoryOne set route through every area and every boss.
    • EndlessA random route that ramps every round, with reward chests and champions. Go alone or bring a friend.
    • DarkEnemies at double strength. Die, and you lose everything you wear and everything you found.
    • The Garbage Man10 waves of garbage, faster every wave. One top-out ends it.

    Chapter V · The Arena

    Duel for the win, or for their gear

    VS

    In the Hollow Spire you pick your opponent off the board. Look over their gear first, then fight for the win, or put a piece of your gear against a piece of theirs. Every hit you land is a line you cleared, so the better stacker usually walks away richer.

    Duels won so far: 54 · Versus rounds won on the Farm: 150
