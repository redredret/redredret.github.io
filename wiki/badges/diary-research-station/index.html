    Research Station Diary

    Research Station Diary is a badge, shown beside your name in chat, on friends' rows and before a duel.

    Goal

    Claim the Easy diary of the Research Station.

    Its colour follows the hardest tier of the Research Station Diary you have finished.

    Every number on this page is read from the game's own data (build a3b0ecdba, 2026-11-27), so it is current with the release it shipped beside. Sections marked Not written yet are waiting for the team's words.
