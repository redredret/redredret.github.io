    Greenshore Fields Diary

    Greenshore Fields Diary is a badge, shown beside your name in chat, on friends' rows and before a duel.

    Goal

    Claim the Easy diary of Greenshore Fields.

    Its colour follows the hardest tier of the Greenshore Fields Diary you have finished.
