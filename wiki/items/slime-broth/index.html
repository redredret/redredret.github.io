    Slime Broth

    Slime Broth is a rare food. Slime gel broth. Tastes better than it looks.

    Effects

    • +8 health when a hit leaves you at half health or less
    • Blocks the next attack on you

    Food: heals 8 health when you fall to 50% or below; the food slot holds 3.
