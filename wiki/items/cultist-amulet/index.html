    Cultist's Amulet

    Cultist's Amulet is a legendary jewellery. A cultist's red idol. Your hits sometimes bring up the Cult Leader's tentacle.

    Effects

    • The tentacle adds 50% to the hit
    • It holds back the next attack half a second
    • Half the chance in a duel
