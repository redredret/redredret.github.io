    Farm versus

    Farm versus: Battle other players. You keep everything you gather.

    Send garbage

    Up to 10 players a room.

    Your clears send garbage to the others. Spins and combos send the most.

    Clearing while garbage waits for you cancels some of it first.

    Last one standing wins the round.

    What clears send

    Public finds you a room. Private gives you a code to share with friends.

    DoubleA Double sends 1.
    QuadA Quad sends 4.
    T-Spin DoubleA T-Spin Double sends 4.
    All ClearAn All Clear sends 10.
    Back-to-BackA hard clear right after another adds 1.
    ComboFrom combo 2, every clear adds more.
