    Ranked Farm

    Ranked Farm

    Ranked Farm is the Farm's one-on-one ladder: a best of three against a player of your rating, a rank from Coal to Darkite, and a pot the winner spins a multiplier for.

    Ranks

    RankShare of playersEntry fee
    Coal18%25 Gold
    Iron20%50 Gold
    Mithril22%100 Gold
    Adamantite18%200 Gold
    Orichalcum12%350 Gold
    Bloodstone7%500 Gold
    Darkite3%750 Gold

    The winner's multiplier
