    Sand Storm Diary

    Sand Storm Diary is the task list for Sand Storm, Easy to Elite, judged from your runs there.

    Easy

    • Defeat 50 Raiders in the Sand Storm.
    • Defeat a Raider with a combo.
    • Open a Sand Storm chest.

    Reward: 2 × Storm Chart.

    Medium

    • Defeat the Scrap Warlord in the Sand Storm.
    • Defeat a Raider with a Back-to-Back clear.
    • Defeat the Scrap Warlord with his crew dead.

    Reward: 5 × Storm Chart and a tier 2 chest.

    Hard

    • Defeat the Scrap Warlord without a Dust Devil ripping a block.
    • Defeat the Scrap Warlord with a T-Spin.
    • Defeat 5 Raiders in a row, each inside a combo of 3 or more.

    Reward: 10 × Storm Chart.

    Elite

    • Defeat the Scrap Warlord with a T-Spin Triple.
    • Defeat the Scrap Warlord within 60 seconds of meeting him.
    • Get through a whole visit to the Sand Storm without eating food.

    Reward: 15 × Storm Chart and a tier 3 chest.

    Every number on this page is read from the game's own data (build 95f2ac7d1, 2026-11-27), so it is current with the release it shipped beside. Sections marked Not written yet are waiting for the team's words.
