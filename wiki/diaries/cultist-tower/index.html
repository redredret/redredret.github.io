    Cultist Tower Diary

    Cultist Tower Diary is the task list for Cultist Tower, Easy to Elite, judged from your runs there.

    Easy

    • Defeat 25 Cultists in Cultist Tower.
    • Defeat a Cultist with a spin.
    • Open a Cultist Tower chest.

    Reward: 2 × Cultist Chart.

    Medium

    • Defeat the Cult Leader in Cultist Tower.
    • Break the Cult Leader's ward with a Quad.
    • Defeat a Cultist before it attacks twice.

    Reward: 5 × Cultist Chart and a tier 2 chest.

    Hard

    • Defeat the Cult Leader with a clear that takes your bottom row.
    • Defeat the Cult Leader with a T-Spin.
    • Defeat the Cult Leader before he raises his second ward.

    Reward: 10 × Cultist Chart.

    Elite

    • Defeat the Cult Leader with a T-Spin Triple that takes your bottom row.
    • Defeat the Cult Leader with 10 health or less.
    • Get through a whole visit to Cultist Tower without taking damage.

    Reward: 15 × Cultist Chart and a tier 3 chest.

    Every number on this page is read from the game's own data (build f9bff5716, 2026-11-27), so it is current with the release it shipped beside. Sections marked Not written yet are waiting for the team's words.
