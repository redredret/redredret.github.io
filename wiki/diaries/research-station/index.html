    Research Station Diary

    Research Station Diary is the task list for Research Station, Easy to Elite, judged from your runs there.

    Easy

    • Defeat 25 Sand Veil Cultists in the Research Station.
    • Defeat a Sand Veil Cultist with a spin.
    • Open a Research Station chest.

    Reward: 2 × Blank Chart.

    Medium

    • Defeat the Lesser Cult Leader in the Research Station.
    • Defeat a Sand Veil Cultist that brands you, without a brand flaring.
    • Defeat 5 Sand Veil Cultists in a row without taking damage.

    Reward: 5 × Blank Chart and a tier 2 chest.

    Hard

    • Defeat the Lesser Cult Leader with a T-Spin.
    • Defeat the Lesser Cult Leader before he raises his second Sun Shield.
    • Defeat the Lesser Cult Leader without his Sun Ring touching you.

    Reward: 10 × Blank Chart.

    Elite

    • Defeat the Lesser Cult Leader with a T-Spin Triple.
    • Get through a whole visit to the Research Station without taking damage.
    • Defeat the Lesser Cult Leader with 10 health or less.

    Reward: 15 × Blank Chart and a tier 3 chest.

    Every number on this page is read from the game's own data (build d64bc5343, 2026-11-28), so it is current with the release it shipped beside. Sections marked Not written yet are waiting for the team's words.
