    Pyramids Diary

    Pyramids Diary is the task list for Pyramids, Easy to Elite, judged from your runs there.

    Easy

    • Defeat 25 Mummies in the Pyramids.
    • Defeat a Mummy with a spin.
    • Open a Pyramids chest.

    Reward: 2 × Pyramid Chart.

    Medium

    • Defeat the Possessed Sarcophagus in the Pyramids.
    • Defeat a Mummy before it binds you.
    • Defeat a Mummy without holding a piece.

    Reward: 5 × Pyramid Chart and a tier 2 chest.

    Hard

    • Defeat the Possessed Sarcophagus with a Quad that takes your bottom row.
    • Defeat the Possessed Sarcophagus without his spirits touching you.
    • Defeat the Possessed Sarcophagus before he raises the dead a second time.

    Reward: 10 × Pyramid Chart.

    Elite

    • Defeat the Possessed Sarcophagus with a T-Spin Triple.
    • Get through a whole visit to the Pyramids without taking damage.
    • Defeat the Possessed Sarcophagus with 10 health or less.

    Reward: 15 × Pyramid Chart and a tier 3 chest.

    Every number on this page is read from the game's own data (build a3b0ecdba, 2026-11-27), so it is current with the release it shipped beside. Sections marked Not written yet are waiting for the team's words.
