    Dust Bowl Diary

    Dust Bowl Diary is the task list for Dust Bowl, Easy to Elite, judged from your runs there.

    Easy

    • Defeat 50 Sand Zombies in the Dust Bowl.
    • Defeat a Wormling with a spin.
    • Open a Dust Bowl chest.

    Reward: 2 × Bowl Chart.

    Medium

    • Defeat the Desert Worm in the Dust Bowl.
    • Defeat a Wormling with a Quad.
    • Defeat 5 Sand Zombies in a row without taking damage.

    Reward: 5 × Bowl Chart and a tier 2 chest.

    Hard

    • Defeat the Desert Worm with a T-Spin.
    • Defeat the Desert Worm with the clear that shoots him out of his burrow.
    • Defeat the Desert Worm before he burrows a second time.

    Reward: 10 × Bowl Chart.

    Elite

    • Defeat the Desert Worm with a T-Spin Triple.
    • Get through a whole visit to the Dust Bowl without taking damage.
    • Defeat the Desert Worm without his burrow eating a block or his chomp touching you.

    Reward: 15 × Bowl Chart and a tier 3 chest.

    Every number on this page is read from the game's own data (build 95f2ac7d1, 2026-11-27), so it is current with the release it shipped beside. Sections marked Not written yet are waiting for the team's words.
