    Bandit Town Diary

    Bandit Town Diary is the task list for Bandit Town, Easy to Elite, judged from your runs there.

    Easy

    • Defeat 50 Bandit Elites in Bandit Town.
    • Defeat a Bandit Elite with a Quad.
    • Open a Bandit Town chest.

    Reward: 2 × Town Chart.

    Medium

    • Defeat a Bandit Elite with a Back-to-Back Quad.
    • Defeat the Bandit King in Bandit Town 5 times.
    • Defeat a Bandit Elite without an arrow hitting you.

    Reward: 5 × Town Chart and a tier 2 chest.

    Hard

    • Defeat the Bandit King with a Quad that takes your bottom row.
    • Defeat the Bandit King within 60 seconds of meeting him.
    • Defeat 5 Bandit Elites in a row without an arrow hitting you.

    Reward: 10 × Town Chart.

    Elite

    • Defeat the Bandit King with a T-Spin Double.
    • Get through a whole visit to Bandit Town without an arrow hitting you.
    • Defeat the Bandit King in Bandit Town without holding a piece.

    Reward: 15 × Town Chart and a tier 3 chest.

    Every number on this page is read from the game's own data (build 95f2ac7d1, 2026-11-27), so it is current with the release it shipped beside. Sections marked Not written yet are waiting for the team's words.
