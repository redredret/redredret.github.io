    Slime Grotto Diary

    Slime Grotto Diary is the task list for Slime Grotto, Easy to Elite, judged from your runs there.

    Easy

    • Defeat the Slime King in Slime Grotto.
    • Open a Slime Grotto chest.
    • Defeat 50 Slimes in Slime Grotto.

    Reward: 2 × Grotto Chart.

    Medium

    • Shake off the Slime King's envelop 3 times in one fight.
    • Defeat the Slime King with a Quad down his four-wide corridor.
    • Defeat an Entling inside a combo of 4 or more.

    Reward: 5 × Grotto Chart and a tier 2 chest.

    Hard

    • Defeat the Slime King after he has walled you in, slimed you and wrapped you up.
    • Defeat the Slime King with none of your clears soaked.
    • Defeat the Slime King with a T-Spin inside his walls.

    Reward: 10 × Grotto Chart.

    Elite

    • Defeat the Slime King with an All Clear inside his walls.
    • Reach a combo of 5 or more against the Slime King.
    • Get through a whole visit to Slime Grotto with no clear soaked.

    Reward: 15 × Grotto Chart and a tier 3 chest.

    Every number on this page is read from the game's own data (build d64bc5343, 2026-11-28), so it is current with the release it shipped beside. Sections marked Not written yet are waiting for the team's words.
