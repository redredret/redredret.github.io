    Sand Cave Diary

    Sand Cave Diary is the task list for Sand Cave, Easy to Elite, judged from your runs there.

    Easy

    • Defeat 25 Rocklings in the Sand Cave.
    • Defeat a Cave Spider with a combo.
    • Open a Sand Cave chest.

    Reward: 2 × Cave Chart.

    Medium

    • Defeat the Sandstone Golem in the Sand Cave.
    • Defeat a Rockling with a Quad.
    • Defeat a Cave Spider before it lays a second egg.

    Reward: 5 × Cave Chart and a tier 2 chest.

    Hard

    • Defeat the Sandstone Golem with a T-Spin.
    • Defeat the Sandstone Golem with a T-Spin Double.
    • Defeat the Sandstone Golem before he splits the ground a second time.

    Reward: 10 × Cave Chart.

    Elite

    • Defeat the Sandstone Golem with a T-Spin Triple.
    • Get through a whole visit to the Sand Cave without your stack reaching 12 rows.
    • Defeat the Sandstone Golem without taking damage or eating food.

    Reward: 15 × Cave Chart and a tier 3 chest.
