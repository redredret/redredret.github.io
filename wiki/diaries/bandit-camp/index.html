    Bandit Camp Diary

    Bandit Camp Diary is the task list for Bandit Camp, Easy to Elite, judged from your runs there.

    Easy

    • Defeat 50 Bandits in Bandit Camp.
    • Defeat a Bandit with a combo.
    • Open a Bandit Camp chest.

    Reward: 2 × Camp Chart.

    Medium

    • Defeat the Bandit King in Bandit Camp.
    • Deflect all six arrows of one arrow rain with your held piece.
    • Defeat 3 Bandits in a row without an arrow hitting you.

    Reward: 5 × Camp Chart and a tier 2 chest.

    Hard

    • Defeat the Bandit King with a T-Spin.
    • Defeat the Bandit King without taking a single arrow from him.
    • Defeat the Bandit King before his second arrow rain.

    Reward: 10 × Camp Chart.

    Elite

    • Defeat the Bandit King with a T-Spin Triple.
    • Defeat the Bandit King without taking damage or eating food.
    • Deflect 24 arrows without getting hit by one, in one fight with the Bandit King.

    Reward: 15 × Camp Chart and a tier 3 chest.

    Every number on this page is read from the game's own data (build 95f2ac7d1, 2026-11-27), so it is current with the release it shipped beside. Sections marked Not written yet are waiting for the team's words.
