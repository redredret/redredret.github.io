    Cyclops Arena Diary

    Cyclops Arena Diary is the task list for Cyclops Arena, Easy to Elite, judged from your runs there.

    Easy

    • Defeat Punchy, Shooty and Bernie in Cyclops Arena.
    • Open a Cyclops Arena chest.
    • Defeat 25 Super Zombies in Cyclops Arena.

    Reward: 2 × Cyclops Chart.

    Medium

    • Defeat Punchy with a Quad.
    • Defeat Shooty with a spin.
    • Defeat Bernie before his fire burns a second block.

    Reward: 5 × Cyclops Chart and a tier 2 chest.

    Hard

    • Defeat Punchy without his slam hitting you.
    • Defeat Bernie with a T-Spin while his fire is still burning.
    • Defeat Shooty with a clear that takes your bottom row.

    Reward: 10 × Cyclops Chart.

    Elite

    • Defeat all three brothers in one visit, none of them touching you.
    • Defeat one of the brothers with a T-Spin Triple.
    • Get through a whole visit to Cyclops Arena without eating food.

    Reward: 15 × Cyclops Chart and a tier 3 chest.

    Every number on this page is read from the game's own data (build d64bc5343, 2026-11-28), so it is current with the release it shipped beside. Sections marked Not written yet are waiting for the team's words.
