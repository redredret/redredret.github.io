    Sand Storm

    Sand Storm is a place in the Red Desert Story and a region of the Endless Dungeon. Raiders in a sand storm.

    In the Red Desert Story

    The second of the Red Desert Story's 6 places. The Story opens once the Greenshore Story is complete.
