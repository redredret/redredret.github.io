    Pyramids

    Pyramids is a place in the Red Desert Story and a region of the Endless Dungeon. Torchlit halls under the sand, where Mummies keep watch over the Possessed Sarcophagus.

    In the Red Desert Story

    The third of the Red Desert Story's 6 places. The Story opens once the Greenshore Story is complete.
