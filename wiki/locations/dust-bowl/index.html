    Dust Bowl

    Dust Bowl is a place in the Red Desert Story and a region of the Endless Dungeon. Red sand and a worm.

    In the Red Desert Story

    The first of the Red Desert Story's 6 places. The Story opens once the Greenshore Story is complete.
