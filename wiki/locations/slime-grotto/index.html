    Slime Grotto

    Slime Grotto is a region of the Endless Dungeon. A wet green cave, slime on every wall. A smaller Slime King holds the pool.

    In the Endless Dungeon

    A tier 5 region of Greenshore. It opens when you reach wave 10 of Slime King or Slime King Coop.
