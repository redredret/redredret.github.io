    Hellish Desert

    Hellish Desert is a place in the Red Desert Story and a region of the Endless Dungeon. The Research Station, turned to hell.

    In the Red Desert Story

    The sixth of the Red Desert Story's 6 places. The Story opens once the Greenshore Story is complete.
