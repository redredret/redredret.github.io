    The Dump

    The Dump is a region of the Endless Dungeon. Heaps of rubbish and the dead that crawl out of them. The Garbage Man works here, slower than at home.

    In the Endless Dungeon

    A tier 4 region of Greenshore. It opens when you reach wave 4 of Garbage Man.
