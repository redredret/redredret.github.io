    Technique

    The moves that clear more and send more.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    Kicks

    If a turn doesn't fit, the game tries four more spots nearby, in order. Moving to one of them is a kick.

    Kicks let you turn a piece into a tight gap.

    The arrow shows where the kick moved the piece.

    T-Spins

    Turn a T into a slot so three of its four corners are blocked.

    The turn has to be your last move before it locks.

    Single, Double and Triple clear one, two and three rows. A Mini is a smaller spin.

    A T-Spin Double sends as much as a Quad.

    All-spins

    Any other piece turned into a spot where it can't move left, right or up is an all-spin.

    S, Z, L, J and I all spin. An all-spin sends as much as a T-Spin.

    The O never spins. It turns in place.

    What spins send

    A spin sends more than a plain clear of the same size.

    T-Spin SingleA T-Spin Single sends 2 garbage. A plain Single sends 0.2
    T-Spin DoubleA T-Spin Double sends 4 garbage. A plain Double sends 1.4
    T-Spin TripleA T-Spin Triple sends 6 garbage. A plain Triple sends 2.6
    T-Spin MiniA Mini still counts as a spin for combos and Back-to-Back.0

    Combos

    Clear with piece after piece. Each clear in a row adds one to the combo.

    From combo 2, each clear sends extra garbage.

    A piece that clears nothing ends it.

    Back-to-Back

    Quads and spin clears are hard clears.

    Hard clears in a row, with no easy clear between, send 1 extra.

    All Clear

    Clear every block on the board with one piece.

    It sends 10 garbage, whatever cleared it.
