    Champions

    Stronger enemies with an extra effect. Some effects help you.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    Where champions appear

    Any fight in the Endless Dungeon or the Dark Endless Dungeon can be a champion, alone or with a partner.

    The chance is 3% in the first round and grows 3% a round, up to 35%.

    A champion wears a ring in its colour, and its name goes before the enemy's.

    The Story, duels and the other dungeons have none.

    Every champion

    Each champion has one of these, on top of the enemy's own attacks.

    VampiricHeals itself for 50% of every hit it lands on you.Harder
    ReinforcedTakes 15% less damage from your hits.Harder
    AgileAttacks 20% faster, but has 20% less health.Harder
    ThornedEvery hit you land on it costs you 2 health. It can't take your last point.Harder
    HulkingHas 30% more health, and attacks 10% slower.Harder
    EnragedIts hits deal 20% more.Harder
    GildedDrops 30 to 80 extra Gold when it dies.Good for you
    HealerHeals you 10 when it dies.Good for you
    BrittleTakes 15% more damage from your hits.Good for you

    What champions drop

    A champion drops its enemy's own loot, and has a chance at these too.

    Gold CoinGilded champions drop 30 to 80 extra.
    Kamikaze Idol1 in 10 champions drop one.
    Scrap1 in 5 champions drop 1 to 3.
    Treasure Seal1 in 20 champions drop one.
    Clutch Idol1 in 250 champions drop one.
    Sand Idol1 in 20 Agile champions drop one.
    Thorny Idol1 in 10 Thorned champions drop one.

    With a partner

    A party meets the same champions, and fights them together.

    A Healer heals everyone still standing when it dies.

    A Vampiric champion heals the enemy you share.

    Champion's Lure counts once for the party, the best one any of you wears.

    Gear for champion hunters

    Three enhancements work against champions.

    Champion's Lure+1% chance each Endless fight is a champion.
    Hunter+10% damage against champions.
    Fortune5% chance a champion drops its loot twice.
