    Duels and the Arena

    Fight another player.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    Player against player

    Your clears hit the other player, and their board takes the hit.

    Topping out costs health. The first to 0 loses.

    The Arena

    Post a fight, or pick one off the board. Check each other's gear, then press FIGHT.

    In a friendly fight, nothing is at stake.

    In a risk fight, the winner takes every item the loser is wearing.

    Once someone joins, you can't change your gear. Leaving or closing the game counts as a loss.

    In the Dark

    In the Dark Endless Dungeon, other players can find you between fights.

    Your gear in a fight

    Your gear, upgrades and enhancements work against players too, most of them at half.

    Each one's own text says what changes in a duel.
