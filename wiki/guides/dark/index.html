    The Dark Endless Dungeon

    DANGER. A riskier, more rewarding endless dungeon.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    Checkpoints

    Every enemy is twice as strong as the Story's, and attacks twice as often.

    Every two rounds you reach a checkpoint. Take the red exit sign out with everything you found, or keep going.

    On a planned trip the checkpoint comes at every region's chest, where you can also stay or open the map.

    Leaving any other way counts as dying.

    Other players

    Other players hunt here too. After your first kill they can find you.

    A fight with them can't be refused, and you can't run from it.

    What you risk

    If killed by a monster, your gear will be BROKEN, and will need to be repaired.

    If killed by a player, YOU LOSE EVERY ITEM YOU HAVE EQUIPPED.

    Either way, every item you found will be lost. You keep your XP.
