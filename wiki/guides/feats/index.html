    Feats, quests and the log

    Goals that pay out, and a record of what you've found.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    Feats

    Reach a feat's goal, then claim it from Feats.

    Greenshore MasterKill 10 Cult Leaders in the Dark. 10% more damage to Cultists and Cult Leaders
    Desert MasterKill 10 Unbound in the Dark. 10% more damage to every kind of cultist
    Garbage GarrisonBeat wave 10 of the Garbage Man's dump. 2× Gold for clearing Farm garbage. 50% off the Garbage Man's door
    Farm FighterClear 10,000 lines on the Farm. +50% of every material from Farm runs
    Master FarmerReach Farm level 19, where the Harvest is ×3.00. Start Solo Farm runs at level 4, and later with each rank. The levels you skip are still paid
    Lord of the HarvestClear 30,000 lines on the Farm. 2× character XP from every Farm run
    Nothing WastedSalvage 200 pieces of gear at the Smelter. +1 Scrap for every tier-two or better piece you salvage
    KingslayerDefeat 100 Slime Kings. The Slime King's pit needs no Slime Key
    Merchant PrinceEarn 50,000 Gold selling to merchants. Merchants pay 25% more for everything you sell them
    Second OpinionTurn in 100 tasks. Swap two daily tasks a day instead of one
    StallholderSell 50 Market listings to other players. 5 more Market listings up at once
    Omen SeekerOpen 30 chests in Endless or Dark. Turn on Omens in Endless and Dark, and in co-op Endless. 20% more XP from every run with Omens on
    Long WalkWalk 7 regions on one planned trip. One more roll in every arrival chest

    Tasks

    Goals that pay out.

    Daily and weekly challenges. Finish one and claim its reward.

    Quests

    Chains of goals from the people of Greenshore. Each one leads to the next.

    Open them with the QUESTS button beside the main menu.
