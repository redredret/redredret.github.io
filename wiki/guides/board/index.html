    The board

    Fill rows to clear them. Don't let the stack reach the top.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    Ten wide, twenty tall

    Pieces drop in from the top, one at a time.

    They come in bags of seven, one of each shape.

    NEXT shows the next 5. The ghost shows where a piece will land.

    Hold

    Hold puts the falling piece aside for later and brings out the next.

    Hold again to swap it back. You can hold once per piece.

    More at once is worth more

    Fill a row from wall to wall and it clears.

    Clear four rows at once for a Quad.

    Points are times your level, which goes up every 10 lines.

    What each clear is worth

    Garbage is what a clear sends to another player, or how hard it hits in a fight.

    SingleA Single scores 100 and sends 0 garbage.100
    DoubleA Double scores 300 and sends 1 garbage.300
    TripleA Triple scores 500 and sends 2 garbage.500
    QuadA Quad scores 800 and sends 4 garbage.800

    Garbage

    Grey rows that push your stack up.

    Garbage rises from the floor. Each row has one hole.

    On the Farm it comes every 20 to 40 pieces. In versus, other players send it.

    It waits in the red meter. A piece that clears lines holds it back. One that clears nothing lets it rise.

    Topping out

    If a new piece has no room to appear, you top out.

    On the Farm that ends the run. In a fight it costs health and clears your board.

    If you cleared a line just before, the new piece gets one lift to safety.
