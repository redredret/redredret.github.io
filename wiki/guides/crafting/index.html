    Crafting, boosts and trading

    Turn what you gather into gear, food and boosts, or trade it for Gold.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    Refining

    Refining takes 25 raw materials for one plank, ingot or fabric.

    Some recipes need a level or a station.

    Crafting is instant. Make as many at once as you can afford.

    Boosts

    Items that help a run, used up as you play.

    Put boosts in the Boosts tab of your inventory.

    A card tells you each time one is used, and how many are left.

    FertiliserBoost. Next Farm run gathers twice the materials.Fertiliser slot
    Treasure SealBoost. Each one makes one chest of an Endless or Dark run one tier better.Seal slot
    Provisions PouchBoost. Your food slot holds one more. Used up by a fight where you carry the extra one.Bag slot
    Bandage RollBoost. The first time your co-op partner goes down, one line clear revives them on full health.Aid slot
    Fields ChartBoost. On WANDER, takes you to Greenshore Fields after your first stretch. Used up there.Map slot

    Selling

    Buy and sell for Gold.

    Shops buy anything for a set price.

    On the Player Market, you set the price and other players buy.

    A sale pays you after a 5% tax, or 3% with the Trading Post.

    Trading opens at level 10, or after two hours of play.
