    Controls and handling

    Change any key in Settings, Controls, and how pieces move in Settings, Handling.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    Moving pieces

    Move, turn, drop and hold.

    The keys under the board light up as they are pressed.

    Every key

    These are your keys right now.

    Move LeftController: D-Pad LeftLeft / A
    Move RightController: D-Pad RightRight / D
    Soft / Sonic DropController: D-Pad DownDown / S
    Hard DropController: YSpace
    Rotate ClockwiseController: AUp / W / X
    Rotate CounterclockwiseController: BQ / Z
    Rotate 180°Controller: RBV
    HoldController: X / LBC / Shift
    PauseController: StartEscape / P
    RestartController: UnboundR
    Show DetailsController: BackTab

    DAS, ARR and SDF

    Tap to move a cell. Hold to slide.

    DAS is how long you hold before a piece starts to slide. 120 ms to start.

    ARR is how fast it slides. 20 ms a cell to start.

    SDF is how much faster soft drop falls. It starts at 20×.

    Change all three in Settings, Handling.

    Soft and hard drop

    Soft drop speeds the piece down, and you can still move it.

    Hard drop puts it straight down and locks it.

    Lock delay

    A piece on the ground doesn't lock straight away.

    A piece locks 0.5 seconds after it lands.

    Each move or turn restarts that clock, up to 15 times.

    Falling lower than it has been gives all 15 back.
