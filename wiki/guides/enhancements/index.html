    Enhancements

    Every enhancement the Forge can roll, and where each one works.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    Enhancing gear

    Roll an enhancement onto a piece at the Scrap Forge. A piece holds one, and a new roll replaces it.

    Each roll on the same piece costs more Scrap than the last.

    Some chests and drops come enhanced already.

    Grades

    Each enhancement comes in grades, I to IV. Better gear rolls higher grades.

    Sturdy gives 50% more on body armor.

    Tier I gearI 75% · II 25%Worn from level 1
    Tier II gearI 40% · II 45% · III 15%Worn from level 10
    Tier III gearI 5% · II 35% · III 45% · IV 15%Worn from level 30
    Tier IV gearII 10% · III 45% · IV 45%Worn from level 35

    In duels and with a partner

    Each enhancement below says what changes in a duel or with a partner.

    Many fight enhancements count at half in a duel, and a few do nothing there.

    A duel has no chests, champions or partners, so enhancements about them never come up.

    Dark Pact counts the checkpoints your Dark run passed before the fight.

    With a partner, yours work on your own board and your own hits, as they do alone.

    Stat enhancements

    These add to your stats, everywhere you fight.

    Keen+1 Attack.
    Heavy+1 Power.
    Honed+1 Melee bonus.
    Sturdy+1 Armor.
    Trained+1 bonus to your weapon's style.
    Balanced+1 Attack, Power and Armor.
    Reckless+1 Attack and Power, -1 Armor.

    Fight enhancements

    These change how a fight goes.

    Cruel4% chance a hit deals 50% more.
    Vampiric8% chance a hit heals you 4.
    Warded5% chance to halve a hit you take.
    ThornedAnything that hits you takes 1 back.
    MendingHeal 2 on each kill.
    Staggering5% chance a hit holds the next attack back 1 second.
    Overkill25% of the damage past a kill hits the next enemy.
    First Strike+10% damage on your first clear against each enemy.
    Savage8% chance a hit rolls its Power twice and keeps the higher.
    Warmonger+5% damage in Onslaught fights.
    Hunter+10% damage against champions.
    FocusedBack-to-back clears heal 1.
    Foresighted5% chance an attack on your board is turned away.
    TacticianTriples and Quads hold the next attack back 0.2 seconds more.
    VigilantThe first hit of each fight deals 20% less.
    Champion's Lure+1% chance each Endless fight is a champion.
    SteadfastReaching a Dark checkpoint heals 5.
    RallyA partner you revive stands up with 5 more health.
    RegeneratingHeal 5 health a minute in a fight.
    Retaliation8% chance to hit back when you are hit.
    Last StandOnce a run, dropping below 20 health heals 10.
    RestedOpening a chest heals 5.
    Ironhide+1 Armor against bosses.
    Rebuttal+10% damage on your next clear after an attack hits your board.
    Reserve+5% damage with a piece from hold.
    Interrupting5% chance a Triple or Quad in the last second stops the attack.
    AegisTake 10% less damage while your co-op partner is down.
    Shield BashBlocking or parrying hits back for 50% of your Attack.
    TranquilHeal 8 for every 100 pieces placed with a stack 4 rows high or less.
    MomentumReaching a 5 combo heals 2.
    TwistingAfter a spin clear, the next hit deals 10% less.
    Dark Pact+2% damage for each Dark checkpoint you pass, up to 3.
    HeartstoneBelow half health, each new fight heals 3.
    Untouched+5% damage until something hits you in a fight.
    Flurry8% chance a combo clear deals 50% more.
    Long Shot+5% damage on Quads.
    Glass+5% damage dealt, +2.5% damage taken.

    Reward enhancements

    These change what a dungeon run pays you.

    Scholar+3% experience.
    Gilded+5% coins.
    Scavenger's2% chance a kill drops 1 Scrap.
    Gambler's Charm1% chance a kill drops its loot twice.
    Fortune5% chance a champion drops its loot twice.
    Blessed+2% chance of a second chest reward.
