    Versus rooms

    Battle other players. You keep everything you gather.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    Send garbage

    Up to 10 players a room.

    Your clears send garbage to the others. Spins and combos send the most.

    Clearing while garbage waits for you cancels some of it first.

    Last one standing wins the round.

    What clears send

    Public finds you a room. Private gives you a code to share with friends.

    DoubleA Double sends 1.1
    QuadA Quad sends 4.4
    T-Spin DoubleA T-Spin Double sends 4.4
    All ClearAn All Clear sends 10.10
    Back-to-BackA hard clear right after another adds 1.+1
    ComboFrom combo 2, every clear adds more.+1 to +5

    Dark Farm

    Versus with stakes.

    Half of everything you gather is put in a pot. Winner takes the pot.

    You only play against other Dark Farm players.
