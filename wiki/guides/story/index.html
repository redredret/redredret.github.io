    Story

    A set road of enemies with a boss at its end.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    Greenshore's road

    Fight every enemy in order. Gold marks a boss.

    Each place you complete joins the Endless Dungeon. Greenshore Fields opens it.

    Finish the whole Story to open the Dark Endless Dungeon and the Red Desert.

    ZombieA shambling corpse that swipes a block off the top of your stack.
    SlimeA knee-high blob of miasma. It coats your piece in slime, and you lose your next rotation.
    SquirrelA squirrel driven mad by the miasma. It nips at you but never touches your board.
    CyclopsA one-eyed brute that knocks blocks loose from the top of your stack, then sets it on fire.
    Oak EntlingA living oak stump. It grows a block on top of one of your columns.
    BanditA Greenshore outlaw whose arrows knock out a block just under the top of your stack.
    Bandit KingThe ruler of Greenshore's outlaws. He switches between a rain of arrows and a blade spin that cuts your top and bottom rows.
    Zombie x2A shambling corpse that swipes a block off the top of your stack.
    Super ZombieA bigger, harder-hitting zombie whose rake tears two or three blocks off your stack.
    PunchyA hulking cyclops whose stone wave sweeps one row of your stack, smashing some blocks and scattering the rest.
    ShootyA cyclops that hurls boulders, dropping three or four stone blocks onto your stack.
    BernieA fire-casting cyclops. Its fireball lights a block in your bottom row, and the fire spreads to the blocks beside it.
    BanditA Greenshore outlaw whose arrows knock out a block just under the top of your stack.
    Bandit Elite x2A Bandit Town veteran. Tougher than a bandit, with the same stack-piercing arrows.
    Cultist x2A robed tower cultist who hurls fireballs at your stack and stabs at its bottom row.
    Cult LeaderThe master of the Cultist Tower. It tears into your stack with portals and fireballs, then shields itself from your next hit.

    The Red Desert's road

    After Greenshore comes the Red Desert. It has six places, each with its own boss.

    Complete a place in the Story and it joins the Endless Dungeon.

    Sand Zombie x2A zombie dried out in the Red Desert. It swipes a block off the top of your stack, twice as hard as its cousin.
    WormlingA young Desert Worm. It eats one block at a time, and every block it eats heals it.
    Desert WormThe Dust Bowl's giant worm. He chomps blocks to heal, then burrows through your tower.
    Raider x2A desert raider paid by the cult to keep travellers out of the storm. It slashes a block off the top of your stack with its scimitar.
    Scrap WarlordThe Raiders' boss. He pays his crew to shoot your board and spins into a dust devil.
    Sand ZombieA zombie dried out in the Red Desert. It swipes a block off the top of your stack, twice as hard as its cousin.
    Mummy x2One of the pyramid's dead, wrapped in linen and floating above the sand. Its bandages bind your HOLD shut until you hit it twice, and it claws at the tower of anyone it has bound.
    Possessed SarcophagusA gilded coffin with a spirit inside. It seals your deepest well with a stone, sends spirits at your tower and raises mummies to take your hits.
    Rockling x2A small rock creature. It drops stones on your tallest column.
    Cave SpiderA pale spider from the Sand Cave. It lays eggs on your stack that hatch and eat blocks.
    Sandstone GolemThe Sand Cave's stone guardian. He cracks and splits your stack, hides your queue in dust and makes your pieces fall fast.
    Sand Veil Cultist x2A veiled cultist from the desert's research station. It hurls fireballs at your stack and strikes at its bottom row with a sunstone staff.
    Lesser Cult LeaderOne of the Sand Veil's officers, who runs the dig. He fights as the Cult Leader does, with portals and fireballs into your stack and then a ward against your next hit.
    The Old ManA frail old man the Sand Veil keep chained at the bottom of their dig. They have studied him for years and never let him die. He doesn't fight back.
    The UnboundThe Old Man, turned into a demon by the gate. He lights wards on your stack, rewrites your next pieces and brands two rows.
