    The Endless Dungeon

    Rounds that never end. The deeper you go, the better the rewards.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    Rounds

    Each round is three enemies, then a boss.

    Every round, enemies get a little stronger and faster.

    Regions

    Each stretch up to a chest is a region, with its own enemies, place and loot.

    Higher tiers are harder and pay better. Find more regions to make your runs richer.

    A chart in your map slot teleports you to its place once. On WANDER you go there after your first stretch.

    Plan a trip

    Plan where your Endless runs go.

    The map opens every time you enter. Pick places one road at a time from Greenshore Fields, then START TRIP.

    Every place is a region you fight through, up to its chest. The desert begins past the Cultist Tower.

    Where the course ends, you choose again at its chest. WANDER takes a random road instead.

    In co-op the host plans the course. That's whoever made the room, or waited longer for a match.

    At every chest

    CONTINUE ON COURSE is already picked, so SPACE keeps you going.

    STAY HERE fights another stretch in the same place. OPEN MAP picks a new direction.

    To walk out with what you found, press the red exit sign in the card's top-right corner.

    On the road

    Reach your destination for an arrival chest, with a roll for every region you walked.

    Chests get better the further you go. A chart takes you straight to its place once.

    You may meet a wanderer from next door. Stay a while and Pressure builds, and the region's bosses come more often.

    Onslaught

    Endless gets faster after round 6.

    From the 25th fight, every round is two enemies and a boss.

    Chests come sooner, and kills give more XP and an extra chance at loot.

    It works the same alone, in the Dark and with a partner.

    Chests

    Some bosses leave a chest behind.

    In Endless, a chest follows the boss of rounds 3, 6, 8, 10 and so on.

    Your next clear opens it, instead of hitting the next enemy.

    Chest tiers

    Deeper regions hold better chests.

    A Treasure Seal in your seal slot raises a chest one tier.

    Wooden ChestThe plainest chest.
    Reinforced ChestTier II.
    Masterwork VaultCan hold an enhanced piece of gear.
    Arcane VaultOnly in tier IV and V regions.
    Royal ChestThe richest chest. It only appears in tier IV and V regions.

    Champions

    Some Endless enemies are stronger, with a twist.

    Any Endless fight can be a champion. The chance grows every round.

    A champion glows in its colour. Most are harder, a few help you.

    Champions can drop extra loot. The Champions page has every one.

    Omens

    Every third chest brings an omen. +20% XP while on.

    Open 30 chests in Endless or the Dark to earn the Omen Seeker feat.

    Then switch omens on or off on the Endless and Dark cards.

    Each omen lasts a number of kills. Gold ones help, red ones hurt, purple ones do both.

    Press Tab in a fight to read your omens again.
