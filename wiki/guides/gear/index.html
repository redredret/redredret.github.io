    Gear and stats

    What you wear decides how hard you hit and how much you take.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    Gear slots

    Drag gear onto a slot to wear it. Press an empty slot to see what fits it.

    Wearing gear needs a level. Most is 1, 10 or 30. A few pieces need 20, 35 or 50.

    You can hold, sell and trade anything at any level.

    HelmetYour helmet. Mostly Armor.
    FoodEaten for you when a hit leaves you at half health or less.
    Body armourYour armour. The most Armor of any slot.
    NecklaceAmulets do something extra, like the King's Amulet.
    WeaponYour weapon decides melee, ranged or magic.
    RingTwo ring slots. Rings do something extra too.
    OffhandA shield, a book or a lantern.
    RingTwo ring slots. Rings do something extra too.

    Six stats

    Your gear decides how hard you hit and how much you take.

    A hit deals your Attack plus a random part of your Power.

    Each level adds a little. Your gear adds the most.

    AttackThe part of every hit you always deal.
    PowerA random extra on every hit, from 0 up to your Power.
    ArmorCuts every hit you take. 5 Armor halves it, 15 cuts it to a quarter.
    Melee bonusAdded to Attack and Power while you hold a melee weapon.
    Ranged bonusAdded to Attack and Power while you hold a bow.
    Magic bonusAdded to Attack and Power while you hold a staff.

    Armor families

    Some of every hit always gets through your Armor.

    Gear is set when a fight starts.

    Iron ArmorHeavy armor has the most Armor, and the smallest Melee bonus.
    Leather TunicLeather is in between, with a Ranged bonus.
    Wizard RobeCloth has the least Armor, and the biggest Magic bonus.

    Rings and amulets

    Jewellery does something beyond its stats.

    You wear one necklace and two rings.

    Most are made from an idol a boss or champion drops.

    Clutched RingA champion's Clutch Idol on a magic ring. Clears from a piece lifted to save you from topping out hit 12.5% harder Half that against a player
    Cultist's AmuletA cultist's red idol. Your hits sometimes bring up the Cult Leader's tentacle. The tentacle adds 50% to the hit It holds back the next attack half a second Half the chance in a duel
    Hourglass AmuletAn Agile champion's idol on a magic chain. A Quad or All Clear delays a monster's next attack an extra second Against a player, a Quad or All Clear heals back half the last hit they landed on you
    Kamikaze NecklaceA champion's idol on a magic chain. It goes off the first time you top out and survive, then breaks. A monster loses a quarter of its full health Another player loses a quarter of what they have left
    King's AmuletThe Bandit King's idol on a magic chain. Enemies that carry coins always drop them, on top of their other drops, with a partner too Against another player, 1 hit in 100 also fires a King's arrow into the top of their tower
    Oozing RingThe Slime King's idol on a magic ring. A T-Spin holds them in slime. A monster's next attack comes two seconds later Another player's next rotation is spent
    Recycler's RingThe Garbage Man's Scrap Idol on a magic ring. A clear that takes out garbage hits 10% harder, and half that against a player.
    Ring of GarbageThe Garbage Man's idol on a magic ring. A clear worth a triple or more also throws garbage. A monster gets a block that holds back its next attack a second Another player gets a line of garbage
    Ring of ThornsA Thorned champion's idol on a magic ring. Thorns never kill. A monster takes back twice the damage it deals you Another player takes back a twentieth of each hit
