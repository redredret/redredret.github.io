    Challenges

    Special dungeons offer unique challenges.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    The Garbage Man's waves

    He throws a line of garbage every 3 seconds at wave 1, and every second by wave 10.

    He does no damage, but topping out ends the run.

    Each time you beat him, your board wipes and he comes back stronger.

    The Garbage Man's door

    It costs 5 Scrap to go in.

    Beat wave 10 for an Arcane Vault, then keep fighting for more.

    The Slime King's tricks

    King after king, each stronger and faster.

    His first move walls your board in to four columns until he falls.

    Slime balls coat your blocks. A clear through slime hits nothing.

    When he wraps you up, move left and right to shake him off.

    Each hit pushes his next attack back only 0.3 seconds.

    The Slime King's pit

    The pit costs a Slime Key to enter. With a friend, you each use your own.

    There is a calm between kings. Dying here loses nothing.

    In the Endless Dungeon's Slime Grotto he is much tougher and drops only what his first king does. Thick Slime, Royal Vaults and most of his idols come from the pit.
