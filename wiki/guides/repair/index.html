    Repair, the Forge and insurance

    Repair, upgrade and insure your gear.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    Mending

    Dying to a monster in the Dark breaks the gear you wear.

    Broken gear stays on you, but works at a tenth of its stats.

    Mend it at the Repair Station with some of what it was made from.

    The Scrap Forge

    Spend Scrap to roll an enhancement onto your gear.

    At the Scrap Forge, each roll puts one enhancement on a piece. Each roll on the same piece costs more.

    Enhancements come in grades I to IV.

    Salvage spare gear at the Smelter for Scrap.

    The Enhancements page has every one.

    The Warden's Vault

    Keep one piece safe.

    Insure one worn piece with Scrap before a Dark run or a risk duel.

    If you die or lose, it stays with you, whole.

    It costs twice what the piece salvages for.

    A Warden's Seal in your seal slot insures your most valuable piece for you.
