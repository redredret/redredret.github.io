    Bosses

    Every boss, weakest first, and every attack it makes.

    Cyclops

    A one-eyed brute that knocks blocks loose from the top of your stack, then sets it on fire.

    Fill around the clump its punch leaves. Don't set a piece on burning blocks, or it burns away.

    Spins hit it 10% harder.
