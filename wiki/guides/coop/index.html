    Co-op

    Two players, one route.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    Fight together

    You each play your own board against the same monster.

    Monsters have 50% more health.

    Drops are split by how much damage each of you deals.

    Your gear and upgrades work as they do alone. The party's best Champion's Lure counts once.

    Getting up again

    If your partner goes down, clear the lines the game asks of you to bring them back.

    Find a partner near your level with Quick Match, or share a private room code.
