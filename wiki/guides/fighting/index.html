    Fighting

    Your clears are your attacks.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    You and the enemy

    Every clear you make hits the enemy.

    It attacks on its own clock. It hits you, and often your board.

    Hit harder

    A hit is worth the garbage its clear would send. A Quad is a full hit, a Single an eighth of one.

    Back-to-back, combos and spins send more, so they hit harder.

    Every hit pushes its next attack back 1 second, never past its full wait.

    Topping out in a fight

    It costs 1 health for each empty cell on your board, then your board clears.

    At 0 health, you lose the fight.

    Weak spots

    Some enemies take 10% more from Triples and Quads, from combos, or from spins. Their page says which.

    Your weapon adds 5% for its own move. Melee likes combos, ranged likes Triples and Quads, magic likes spins.

    Food

    You have 100 health in every fight.

    Put food in your food slot before a fight.

    When a hit leaves you at half health or less, you eat one.

    The slot holds 3. A Provisions Pouch in your bag slot adds one more.
