    Levels and upgrades

    Clears and kills give XP. Levels give points to spend.

    From the game's own How to Play guide (HELP in the main menu), where every picture plays.

    XP

    Every line you clear gives 1 XP. On the Farm, a spin, Quad or All Clear gives at least 2.

    Kills give XP too, and harder modes give more.

    Level 2Level 2 takes 100 XP in all.100
    Level 10Level 10 takes 1,172 XP in all.1,172
    Level 20Level 20 takes 3,663 XP in all.3,663
    Level 30Level 30 takes 8,920 XP in all.8,920
    Level 40Level 40 takes 20,818 XP in all.20,818
    Level 50Level 50 takes 49,712 XP in all.49,712

    What a level gives

    1 Combat point and 1 Skills point every level, and a Build point every third level.

    Higher levels open gear, recipes and stations.

    Combat upgrades

    Spend Combat points to change how fights go.

    Each path starts with one upgrade and opens more as you rank it up.

    Open the Combat tree from Upgrades.

    TechniqueSpin clears, Quads and All Clears deal +2% damage per rank. Half in duels.
    Stand FirmA board break costs you 15% less health per rank.
    Melee Mastery+2 Melee Bonus per rank. Your melee weapons hit harder.
    Ranged Mastery+2 Ranged Bonus per rank. Your bows hit harder.
    Magic Mastery+2 Magic Bonus per rank. Your staffs hit harder.
    LifelineIn co-op, S-Spin and Z-Spin clears heal your partner 2 health per line, per rank.

    Farm skills

    Spend Skills points to gather more.

    Harvest gathers more. Technique pays for unusual clears. Tempo pays for long, steady runs.

    Some skills pay for each line's own spin. Wood likes the L, crops the J, fish the S or Z, and ore the I.

    Bountiful Harvest+5% chance per rank to gather an extra material on the Farm.
    Keen EdgeSpin clears with any piece except T harvest 1 extra material per rank.
    MomentumClears made at a combo of 3 or more harvest 1 extra material per rank.

    Build stations

    Build stations on your Farm.

    Build points and materials buy stations. Many also need a level.

    Stations open rarer materials, new recipes and new services.

    Base Level 1Builds your first base. Unlocks the level 2 Mines, Trees, Pond and Plants upgrades, and the Training Post.
    CookhouseUnlocks cooking Carrot Stew, Trout Pie and Slime Broth for your food slot.
    SmelterSalvage spare gear from your bag into Scrap.
    Master RefineryPlanks, ingots and fabric take 20 raw materials instead of 25 Unlocks Transmutes, which turn materials up a tier for Scrap
    SealwrightUnlocks crafting the Warden's Seal and the Treasure Seal.
    Cartographer's TableDraw charts of regions you've found. A chart takes you straight to its region once
    Warden's VaultInsure one worn piece with Scrap before a Dark run or a risk duel. If you die or lose, it stays with you.
    Scrap ForgeSpend Scrap to roll a modifier onto a piece of gear. Each roll on the same piece costs more.
    Repair StationRepair broken gear worn from below level 30. A repair costs a tenth of the piece's main material and its salvage in Scrap.
    Trading PostThe market takes 3% of your sales instead of 5%, and you can keep 30 listings up instead of 20.
