    Block skins

    Block skins change how your blocks look. Some open with your level, some with a challenge.

    SkinLookHow to get it
    BevelChunky arcade blocks with a lit, bevelled edge.Clear 10 Quads in one run.
    CelestialStarry blocks edged in gold.Reach level 50
    ChalkThe default blocks on dark slate.Yours from the start
    ChromePolished metal plates, riveted at the corners.Win a duel against someone 5 or more levels above you.
    8-BitBig pixels, two colours each.Build a 12 combo in one run.
    Gel PacksThin plastic pouches full of gel. They squish when they land.Play a Slime King run until it ends on its own.
    GemstoneCut-jewel blocks on a velvet board.Reach level 10
    GlacierIce blocks with a cold light inside.Defeat 25 enemies in one Dark Endless run.
    GummySoft, see-through candy blocks.Get 2 Perfect Clears in one run.
    HologramShimmering foil in a silver edge.Reach fight 30 alone in the Endless Dungeon.
    Neon ArcadeNeon tube blocks on a glowing board.Reach level 20
    OrigamiFolded paper in soft colours.Clear lines with 10 non-T spins in one run.
    PocketBoxy blocks in shades of handheld-screen green.Reach level 10 in a Farm run without using hold.
    RunestoneStone blocks with glowing runes. Garbage is plain rock.Reach level 40
    Stained GlassStained-glass blocks, lit from behind.Reach level 30
    ToyboxBright, rounded toy blocks on a deep ink board.Yours from the start
    VectorWireframe cubes drawn in light.Land 5 T-Spin Triples in one run.
    VoxelSolid 3D cubes.Beat the Garbage Man's wave 10.

    Every number on this page is read from the game's own data (build a3b0ecdba, 2026-11-27), so it is current with the release it shipped beside. Sections marked Not written yet are waiting for the team's words.
