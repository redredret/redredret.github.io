    Veteran Gatherer

    Veteran Gatherer is a node on the Skills atlas. Higher chance of tier-four materials on every line, growing with your level.

    Each rank

    • +0.5% per rank at level 50
    • Up to +2% per rank from level 65
    • Requires the level-four stations.

    Needs first

    • Seasoned Gatherer at rank 3
    • Character level 50
