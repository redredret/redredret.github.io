    Omen Seeker

    Omen Seeker is a feat: a permanent perk, claimed once its goal is reached.

    Requirement

    Open 30 chests in Endless or Dark.

    Reward

    • Turn on Omens in Endless and Dark, and in co-op Endless
    • 20% more XP from every run with Omens on
