    Vampiric

    Vampiric is a champion: an ordinary enemy in the Endless Dungeon or Dark can arrive as a Vampiric one.

    What it changes

    Heals itself for 50% of every hit it lands on you.

    The game's advice: Every hit it lands heals it. Block, Parry or Dodge, or finish it fast.
