    The Warlord's Iron

    The Warlord's Iron is a quest line given by Brann. Brann wants the Scrap Warlord's horned helm taken off him properly.

    How it opens

    • Finish the Greenshore Story.
    • Reach level 45.
    • Reach Bounty rank 3.
