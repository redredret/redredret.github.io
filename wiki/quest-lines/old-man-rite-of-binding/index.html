    The Rite of Binding

    The Rite of Binding is a quest line given by The Old Man. The Old Man wants the rite that bound him carried out of hell.

    How it opens

    • Finish the Greenshore Story.
    • Reach level 55.
    • Finish the Red Desert Story.
