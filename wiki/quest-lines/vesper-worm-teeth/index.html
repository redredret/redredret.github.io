    The Worm's Teeth

    The Worm's Teeth is a quest line given by Vesper. Vesper knows how to get the fangs out of the Desert Worm.

    How it opens

    • Finish the Greenshore Story.
    • Reach level 40.
    • Defeat the Desert Worm 5 times.
