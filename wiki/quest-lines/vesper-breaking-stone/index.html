    Breaking Stone

    Breaking Stone is a quest line given by Vesper. Vesper knows where the Sandstone Golem's stone gives way.

    How it opens

    • Finish the Greenshore Story.
    • Reach level 45.
    • Defeat the Sandstone Golem 3 times.
