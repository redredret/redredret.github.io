    Sun and Sand

    Sun and Sand is a quest line given by Katherine. Katherine wants the Sand Veil's sun magic understood.

    How it opens

    • Finish the Greenshore Story.
    • Reach level 48.
    • Defeat the Lesser Cult Leader 3 times.
