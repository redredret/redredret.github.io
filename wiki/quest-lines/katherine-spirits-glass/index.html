    Spirits in the Glass

    Spirits in the Glass is a quest line given by Katherine. Katherine wants to know what got into the tombs of the Pyramids.

    How it opens

    • Finish the Greenshore Story.
    • Reach level 45.
    • Finish the Red Desert Story.
