    Rockling

    Rockling is a monster met in Sand Cave. A small rock creature. It drops stones on your tallest column.

    Attacks

    AttackStyleWhat it does
    RockslideRangedDrops a stone on top of your tallest column. Keep your stack flat so there is no tower for it to grow.

    Weakness: Triples and Quads hit it 10% harder.
