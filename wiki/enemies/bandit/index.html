    Bandit

    Bandit is a monster met in Greenshore, Bandit Camp and Bandit Town. A Greenshore outlaw whose arrows knock out a block just under the top of your stack.

    Attacks

    AttackStyleWhat it does
    Bandit ArrowRangedShoots out a block just under your surface, leaving a hole. Fill the hole before you build over it.

    Weakness: combos hit it 10% harder.
