    Cave Spider

    Cave Spider is a monster met in Sand Cave. A pale spider from the Sand Cave. It lays eggs on your stack that hatch and eat blocks.

    Attacks

    AttackStyleWhat it doesOn the board
    Egg SacMeleeLays an egg on your surface. Clear its row within 10 seconds, or it hatches, eats the blocks beside it and bites.▶
    Spider BiteMeleeWhile her egg waits, she bites you instead of laying another.▶

    Each clip is the real fight's board, filmed from the game. It plays once it scrolls into view.

    The game's advice: Clear her egg's row in time, and it never hatches.

    Weakness: combos hit it 10% harder.
