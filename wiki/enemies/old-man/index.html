    The Old Man

    The Old Man is a boss met in Research Station. A frail old man the Sand Veil keep chained at the bottom of their dig. They have studied him for years and never let him die. He doesn't fight back.

    Attacks

    It never attacks.

    The game's advice: He never attacks, so take your time and clear cleanly.

    Weakness: Triples and Quads hit it 10% harder.
