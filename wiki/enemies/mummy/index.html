    Mummy

    Mummy is a monster met in Pyramids. One of the pyramid's dead, wrapped in linen and floating above the sand. Its bandages bind your HOLD shut until you hit it, and it claws at the tower of anyone it has bound.

    Attacks

    AttackStyleWhat it doesOn the board
    Bandage BindMeleeBandages lock your HOLD until you hit it once. Hold a good piece before it wraps you.▶
    Mummy SwipeMeleeWhile you are bound, it swipes a block off your top row. Break the bandages with quick clears.▶

    Each clip is the real fight's board, filmed from the game. It plays once it scrolls into view.

    The game's advice: Its bandages lock your HOLD until you hit it. Hold a good piece before it wraps you, and keep a clear ready to break free.

    Weakness: combos hit it 10% harder.
