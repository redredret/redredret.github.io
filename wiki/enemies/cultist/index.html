    Cultist

    Cultist is a monster met in Greenshore and Cultist Tower. A robed tower cultist who hurls fireballs at your stack and stabs at its bottom row.

    Attacks

    AttackStyleWhat it doesOn the board
    Cultist Fireball VolleyMagicThree fireballs each burn a block or two out of your stack. Fill the holes they leave.▶
    Cultist Kris StabMeleeStabs a block out of your bottom row. If that empties the row, your tower drops a row. Keep the bottom row full.▶

    Each clip is the real fight's board, filmed from the game. It plays once it scrolls into view.

    The game's advice: If its stab empties your bottom row, the tower drops a row. Keep the bottom row full.

    Weakness: spins hit it 10% harder.
