    Slime King

    Slime King is a boss met in Slime Grotto and the Slime King's pit. The king of the slimes. He walls your board in to four columns, slimes your tower and wraps you up.

    Attacks

    AttackStyleWhat it doesOn the board
    Slime WallsMeleeHis walls slam in, and your board is 4 columns wide until he falls. Build flat inside them.▶
    Slime BallsMeleeSlimes two or three blocks on top of your stack. A clear through slime is soaked up, so clear away from it.▶
    EnvelopedMeleeWraps you in slime that drains a point of health every half second. It can't take your last point. Move left and right 6 times to shake it off.▶

    Each clip is the real fight's board, filmed from the game. It plays once it scrolls into view.

    The game's advice: Clear away from slimed blocks, which soak up your hits. Move left and right to break free when he wraps you up.

    Weakness: combos hit it 10% harder.
