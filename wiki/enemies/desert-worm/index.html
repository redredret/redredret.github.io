    Desert Worm

    Desert Worm is a boss met in Dust Bowl. The Dust Bowl's giant worm. He chomps blocks to heal, then burrows through your tower.

    Attacks

    AttackStyleWhat it doesOn the board
    Worm ChompMeleeBites one to four blocks out of your tower, and heals 2% for each. Fill the hole he leaves.▶
    Worm BurrowMeleeHe digs through your tower for up to 12 seconds, eating blocks as he goes. Only a line cleared through his dirt hurts him, and it hits hard.▶

    Each clip is the real fight's board, filmed from the game. It plays once it scrolls into view.

    The game's advice: His chomp heals him. When he burrows, only a line cleared through his dirt hurts him, and it hurts a lot.

    Weakness: combos hit it 10% harder.
