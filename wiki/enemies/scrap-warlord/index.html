    Scrap Warlord

    Scrap Warlord is a boss met in Sand Storm. The Raiders' boss. He pays his crew to shoot your board and spins into a dust devil.

    Attacks

    AttackStyleWhat it doesOn the board
    Pay The CrewRangedHis crew shoot two bolts in under your surface. Clear a line while his coin is in the air and the crew dies for good.▶
    Dust DevilMeleeA dust devil rips 2 blocks off the side of your tower every 3 seconds. Any hit stops it.▶

    Each clip is the real fight's board, filmed from the game. It plays once it scrolls into view.

    The game's advice: Clear a line while his coin is in the air and his crew dies for good. Any hit stops his Dust Devil, so keep a clear ready.

    Weakness: combos hit it 10% harder.
