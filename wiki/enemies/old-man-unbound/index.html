    The Unbound

    The Unbound is a boss met in Hellish Desert. The Old Man, turned into a demon by the gate. He lights wards on your stack, rewrites your next pieces and brands two rows.

    Attacks

    AttackStyleWhat it doesOn the board
    Ward SigilMagicLights an outline on your stack in the shape of a piece you have coming. Lock that piece into it within 8 seconds to stun him and land your next hit twice. Miss it and he strikes three times as hard.▶
    RendMagicTears two or three blocks off the top of your stack.▶
    Hell's BagMagicRewrites your next three pieces. HOLD is the way round it.▶
    Brand Of SinMagicRaises two rows with a key's shape cut through both. Clear both rows with one piece to land your next hit twice. Left, they harden into brimstone.▶
    DemandMagicDemands three clears in a row within 14 seconds. Make them to stun him and land that hit twice. Miss, and a row of hell-iron rises under your stack that takes two fills of its gap to break.▶
    HellblightMagicTurns blocks on your stack to brimstone. Every brimstone block left makes his hits harder, so clear them.▶
    DoomMagicDooms his next hit to land twice as hard. Clear three lines or any spin before it comes to break it, and that clear hits twice.▶
    Soul BurnMagicBurns you for his smallest hit every 2 seconds, up to five times, until you clear any line.▶

    Each clip is the real fight's board, filmed from the game. It plays once it scrolls into view.

    The game's advice: He changes twice as he weakens. Answer each of his moves and your next hit lands double.

    Weakness: spins hit it 10% harder.
