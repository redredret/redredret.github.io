    What Came Through

    What Came Through is step 3 of What the Gate Kept, given by The Old Man.

    Objective

    Defeat the Unbound in the Endless.

    Some of it is still out there. Find it and put it down for good.The Old Man

    Requirements

    • Turn in The Gold Mask first.
    • It can be worked on before level 55, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Gold Coin ×4,000
    • Respec Shard

    7,575 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find The Unbound in the Endless Dungeon or its Dark:
      • the Endless Dungeon's Hellish Desert: a round boss; the warden its chest follows
    2. Defeat The Unbound once in an Endless Dungeon or Dark run (a Story kill does not count).
    3. Turn it in to The Old Man on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • A Story kill does not count. Plan an Endless course to the Hellish Desert on the world map.
    • The Unbound answers to the same tricks as in the Story: see The Unbound.
