    Any Piece Will Do

    Any Piece Will Do is step 7 of The Drillmaster, given by Ines.

    Objective

    Land 30 spin clears with an S, Z, J, L or I.

    The T isn't the only piece that spins. Show me spin clears with the others.Ines

    Requirements

    • Turn in Clean Sweep first.
    • It can be worked on before level 18, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards
