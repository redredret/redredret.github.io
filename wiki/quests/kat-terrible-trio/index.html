    The Terrible Trio

    The Terrible Trio is step 4 of Trouble in Greenshore, given by Katherine.

    Objective

    Defeat Punchy, Shooty and Bernie.

    There are three of them, and they never split up. Beat all three.Katherine

    Requirements

    • Turn in Uncrowned first.
    • It can be worked on before level 10, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Mithril Helmet

    265 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find Punchy:
      • Greenshore, in the Greenshore Story (fight 11 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Cyclops Arena: a round boss; the warden its chest follows
    2. Find Shooty:
      • Greenshore, in the Greenshore Story (fight 12 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Cyclops Arena: a round boss; the warden its chest follows
    3. Find Bernie:
      • Greenshore, in the Greenshore Story (fight 13 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Cyclops Arena: a round boss; the warden its chest follows
    4. Defeat each of Punchy, Shooty and Bernie once.
    5. Turn it in to Katherine on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • All three are on the Greenshore road; the walkthrough above says which fights.
    • Punchy's stone wave sweeps one full row. Clear rows quickly so there is less to smash. He is weak to Triples and Quads.
    • Shooty drops three or four stones on your stack. Keep some room up there and dig them out with your next clears. Weak to spins.
    • Bernie's fire starts in your bottom row and spreads. Clear that row and it goes out. Weak to spins.
