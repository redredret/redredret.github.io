    Unbound

    Unbound is the first of Spirits in the Glass, given by Katherine.

    Objective

    Break a Bandage Bind before it swipes you.

    The Mummies bind your HOLD so you can't use it. Hit one before it swipes and the bandages fall off. Do it three times.Katherine

    Requirements

    • Finish the Greenshore Story.
    • Reach level 45.
    • Finish the Red Desert Story.
    • It can be worked on before level 50, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Gold Coin ×2,000

    6,985 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find Mummy:
      • Pyramids, in the Red Desert Story (fights 2, 3 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Pyramids: an ordinary fight, 50% of them
    2. ▶Wait for Mummy's Bandage Bind. Bandages lock your HOLD until you hit it once. Hold a good piece before it wraps you.
    3. Do it 3 times in all: Break a Mummy's Bandage Bind before it swipes you, 3 times. Every answer counts, in any mode, over as many fights as it takes.
    4. Turn it in to Katherine on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • Hold a good piece before the Mummy wraps you: its bandages lock your HOLD.
    • Break the bandages with a quick clear before it swipes. A small one is enough.
    • The Pyramids Story road has two Mummies; an Endless run through the Pyramids deals more.
