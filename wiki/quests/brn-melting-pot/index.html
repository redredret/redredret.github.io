    Melting Pot

    Melting Pot is the first of Scrap and Sparks, given by Brann.

    Objective

    Break down 30 pieces of gear at the Smelter.

    Gear you've outgrown is Scrap you haven't melted yet. Bring the Smelter thirty pieces.Brann

    Requirements

    • Reach level 40.
    • It can be worked on before level 40, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Adamantite Ingot ×6
    • Riveted Plate ×2

    1,855 experience.
