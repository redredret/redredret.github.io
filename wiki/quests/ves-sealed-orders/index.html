    Sealed Orders

    Sealed Orders is the first of Maps and Seals, given by Vesper.

    Objective

    Make a Warden's Seal at the Sealwright.

    Every long trip I take starts with a seal. Build a Sealwright and make one. You'll see why.Vesper

    Requirements

    • Reach level 24.
    • It can be worked on before level 26, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Rugged Hide ×4
    • Gold Coin ×300

    290 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Get Scrap ×8: dropped by Scrap Warlord (1/2 a roll, 4–8 at a time), Raider (1/19 a roll, 1–2 at a time) or Garbage Man (1/400 a roll, 20–30 at a time); in the region chests of The Dump, Slime Grotto or Sand Storm; a reward from Taking Out the Trash or First Spark.
    2. Get Rough Leather ×4: dropped by Bernie (1/5 a roll), Garbage Man (1/5 a roll, 1–2 at a time) or Punchy (1/5 a roll); crafted from level 10 from Leather ×25; in the region chests of Cyclops Arena; in a tier 1 Endless chests.
    3. Get Eye Ball ×3: dropped by Bernie (1/3 a roll), Cyclops (1/3 a roll) or Punchy (1/3 a roll); in the region chests of Greenshore Fields or Cyclops Arena; in a tier 2 Endless chests.
    4. Get Gold Coin ×300: dropped by Cave Spider (66.67% a roll, 45–70 at a time), Lesser Cult Leader (66.67% a roll, 80–140 at a time) or Mummy (66.67% a roll, 45–70 at a time); in the region chests of Bandit Camp; in a tier 1 Endless chest or a tier 2 Endless chests; a reward from Into Greenshore or Last Rites.
    5. Get Coal ×10: dropped by Desert Worm (40% a roll, 2–4 at a time), Garbage Man (30% a roll, 3–8 at a time) or Sandstone Golem (18.35% a roll, 3–6 at a time); bought for 10 Gold at Katherine's General Goods; in the region chests of The Dump, Sand Cave or Hellish Desert; in a tier 1 Endless chests; a reward from Smelter or Put It to Work.
    6. Craft Warden's Seal at the Sealwright: it needs level 26.
    7. Turn it in to Vesper on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • Build the Sealwright first, from the Build atlas. It opens at level 26.
    • A Warden's Seal takes Scrap, so build the Smelter and break down some spare gear before you start.
