    Forged Horns

    Forged Horns is step 4 of The Warlord's Iron, given by Brann.

    Objective

    Craft the Orichalcum Warhelm.

    His helm, my metal. Melt them together and wear it.Brann

    Requirements

    • Turn in Broken Horns first.
    • It can be worked on before level 50, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Respec Shard ×2

    2,330 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. The recipe opens when Broken Horns is turned in, the step before this one.
    2. Get Warlord's Horned Helm ×1, the piece the recipe upgrades in place (it keeps its Forge roll and ornament): dropped by Scrap Warlord (1/159 a roll).
    3. Get Orichalcum Helmet ×1: dropped by Sandstone Golem (1/500 a roll); crafted from level 50 from Orichalcum Ingot ×5.
    4. Craft Orichalcum Warhelm: it needs level 50.
    5. Turn it in to Brann on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • The Warlord's Horned Helm is a rare drop from the Scrap Warlord. The helm you upgrade keeps its Forge roll and ornament.
    • The Orichalcum Helmet can be made, or taken as the reward for Katherine's The Prisoner.
