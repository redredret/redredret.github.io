    A Good Boy

    A Good Boy is step 2 of The Homestead, given by Hollis.

    Objective

    Finish 3 Farm runs of 10 lines or more.

    One of the farm dogs won't stop following you around. His name's Cubey. Finish three Farm runs and he's yours. Put him in your Pet slot.Hollis

    Requirements

    • Turn in First Harvest first.
    • It can be worked on before level 3, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Cubey

    55 experience.
