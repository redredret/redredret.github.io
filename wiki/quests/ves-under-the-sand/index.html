    Under the Sand

    Under the Sand is the first of The Worm's Teeth, given by Vesper.

    Objective

    Shoot the Desert Worm out of his burrow.

    I need the Worm's fangs for barbs. When he goes underground, clear a row his trail crosses and he comes flying out. Do it three times.Vesper

    Requirements

    • Finish the Greenshore Story.
    • Reach level 40.
    • Defeat the Desert Worm 5 times.
    • It can be worked on before level 45, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Gold Coin ×1,500

    4,370 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find Desert Worm:
      • Dust Bowl, in the Red Desert Story (fight 4 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Dust Bowl: a round boss; the warden its chest follows
    2. ▶Wait for Desert Worm's Worm Burrow. He digs through your tower for up to 12 seconds, eating blocks as he goes. Only a line cleared through his dirt hurts him, and it hits hard.
    3. Do it 3 times in all: Shoot the Desert Worm out of his burrow 3 times. Every answer counts, in any mode, over as many fights as it takes.
    4. Turn it in to Vesper on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • While the Desert Worm is underground, clear a row his trail crosses and he comes flying out. Keep a row or two nearly full low in your stack so you can.
    • Answers count across fights and runs. The Dust Bowl Story road has him once; an Endless Dungeon run through the Dust Bowl can meet him again.
