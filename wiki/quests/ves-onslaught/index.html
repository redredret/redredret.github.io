    Onslaught

    Onslaught is step 4 of The Endless Road, given by Vesper.

    Objective

    Reach encounter 25 in Endless or its Dark.

    Exactly: Reach fight 25 of the Endless Dungeon.

    After the twentieth fight, they come in threes instead of fours. Make it to twenty-five.Vesper

    Requirements

    • Turn in Champion Hunter first.
    • It can be worked on before level 20, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Horn Chunks ×2
    • Mithril Ingot ×8

    910 experience.
