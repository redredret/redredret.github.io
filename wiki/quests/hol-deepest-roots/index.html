    Deepest Roots

    Deepest Roots is step 10 of The Homestead, given by Hollis.

    Objective

    Build any level-four station.

    Exactly: Own 1 of: Mines Lv 4, Trees Lv 4, Plants Lv 4.

    Purple ore, black trees, lotus on the water. It's all down there somewhere. Build a station to level four.Hollis

    Requirements

    • Turn in Master Builder first.
    • It can be worked on before level 50, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Adamantite Ingot ×8
    • Ipe Plank ×8
    • Nettle Fabric ×8

    6,985 experience.
