    First Spark

    First Spark is step 3 of Scrap and Sparks, given by Brann.

    Objective

    Roll a piece of gear at the Scrap Forge.

    Now the hard part. Build the Forge and roll something. Most of the time it gives you nothing much. Sometimes it sings.Brann

    Requirements

    • Turn in Taking Out the Trash first.
    • It can be worked on before level 45, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Scrap ×40
    • Gold Coin ×2,000
