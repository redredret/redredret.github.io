    Lay Them to Rest

    Lay Them to Rest is step 3 of Spirits in the Glass, given by Katherine.

    Objective

    Defeat the Sarcophagus with a Back-to-Back.

    Two hard clears in a row, spins or Quads, hit harder than either alone. Finish him with one. Then I'll show you how to catch a spirit in a lens.Katherine

    Requirements

    • Turn in The Lid first.
    • It can be worked on before level 50, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Gold Coin ×3,000

    6,985 experience.

    Teaches

    • Spiritsight Hat: the recipe can be made once this quest is turned in.
