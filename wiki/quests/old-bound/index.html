    Bound

    Bound is step 3 of The Rite of Binding, given by The Old Man.

    Objective

    Craft the Unbound Focus.

    Bind it. Then it is yours, and not theirs.The Old Man

    Requirements

    • Turn in Unwritten first.
    • It can be worked on before level 55, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Respec Shard ×2

    3,790 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. The recipe opens when Unwritten is turned in, the step before this one.
    2. Get Lotus Tome ×1, the piece the recipe upgrades in place (it keeps its Forge roll and ornament): crafted from level 50 from Lotus Cloth ×4, Darkwood Plank ×3 and Orichalcum Ingot ×1.
    3. Get Cultist's Focus ×1: crafted from level 40 from Cultist Book ×1, Smoked Lens ×1 and Eye Ball ×15.
    4. Get Spider Silk ×20: dropped by Cave Spider (1/11 a roll, 1–2 at a time); in the region chests of Sand Cave.
    5. Get Binding Page ×1: dropped by The Unbound (1/400 a roll).
    6. Craft Unbound Focus: it needs level 55.
    7. Turn it in to The Old Man on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • The Lotus Tome is the book you upgrade: it keeps its Forge roll and ornament.
    • The Binding Page is bound, so you must find it yourself, from the The Unbound. Spider Silk comes from Cave Spiders.
