    On Demand

    On Demand is the first of Hell's Bargain, given by Brann.

    Objective

    Meet the Unbound's Demand 3 times.

    When he demands three clears in a row, make them. That stuns him. Do it three times.Brann

    Requirements

    • Finish the Greenshore Story.
    • Reach level 55.
    • Finish the Red Desert Story.
    • It can be worked on before level 55, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Gold Coin ×3,000

    3,790 experience.
