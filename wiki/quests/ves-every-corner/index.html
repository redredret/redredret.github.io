    Every Corner

    Every Corner is the first of The Far Edge, given by Vesper.

    Objective

    Find every Endless Dungeon region.

    Exactly: Find every one of: Greenshore Fields, Bandit Camp, Bandit Town, Cyclops Arena, Cultist Tower, The Dump, Slime Grotto, Dust Bowl, Sand Storm, Pyramids, Sand Cave, Research Station, Hellish Desert.

    I've walked every one of them. Have you?Vesper

    Requirements

    • Reach level 60.
    • It can be worked on before level 60, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Orichalcum Ingot ×6
    • Darkwood Plank ×6
    • Lotus Cloth ×6

    12,545 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Reach every one of these in an Endless Dungeon or Dark run:
      • Greenshore Fields: it opens when you clear it in the Greenshore Story
      • Bandit Camp: it opens when you clear it in the Greenshore Story
      • Bandit Town: it opens when you clear it in the Greenshore Story
      • Cyclops Arena: it opens when you clear it in the Greenshore Story
      • Cultist Tower: it opens when you clear it in the Greenshore Story
      • The Dump: it opens when you reach wave 4 of Garbage Man
      • Slime Grotto: it opens when you reach wave 10 of Slime King or Slime King Coop
      • Dust Bowl: it opens when you clear it in the Red Desert Story
      • Sand Storm: it opens when you clear it in the Red Desert Story
      • Pyramids: it opens when you clear it in the Red Desert Story
      • Sand Cave: it opens when you clear it in the Red Desert Story
      • Research Station: it opens when you clear it in the Red Desert Story
      • Hellish Desert: it opens when you clear it in the Red Desert Story
    2. Plan a course to it on the world map before the run, or keep going until the road takes you there.
    3. Turn it in to Vesper on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • Regions you found before this quest count.
    • The desert's regions only appear in the Endless Dungeon after you have cleared each of them in the Red Desert Story.
