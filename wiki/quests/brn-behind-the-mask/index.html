    Behind the Mask

    Behind the Mask is step 3 of The Ancient Mask, given by Brann.

    Objective

    Bring Brann the charged Desert Orb.

    Exactly: Hand over Desert Orb ×1.

    We can learn a lot about how hell's magic is influencing the creatures with this orb. For this, you can use my master's forge.Brann

    Requirements

    • Turn in Souls for the Orb first.
    • It can be worked on before level 55, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Gold Coin ×5,000

    7,575 experience.

    Teaches

    • Ancient Mask: the recipe can be made once this quest is turned in.
    • Ancient Warhelm: the recipe can be made once this quest is turned in.

    Walkthrough

    1. Have Desert Orb with you: the one Grave Goods found, charged.
    2. Hand it over to Brann: turning the step in takes it.

    Tips

    • The charged orb counts in your bag or your QUEST slot. Turn the step in between runs, not during one.
    • Turning it in teaches the Ancient Mask and the Ancient Warhelm.
