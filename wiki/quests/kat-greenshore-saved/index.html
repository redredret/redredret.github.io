    Greenshore Saved

    Greenshore Saved is step 6 of Trouble in Greenshore, given by Katherine.

    Objective

    Complete the Greenshore Story.

    Exactly: Finish the Greenshore Story.

    See it through to the end. The whole town is counting on you, and so am I.Katherine

    Requirements

    • Turn in Last Rites first.
    • It can be worked on before level 12, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Mithril Armor

    410 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Greenshore: 19 fights, ending with Cyclops, Bandit King, Punchy, Shooty, Bernie and Cult Leader.
    2. Beat the last of them and the Story is complete.
    3. Turn it in to Katherine on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • Each boss on the road has its own page, with its attacks shown on the board.
    • Put food in your food slot before a long road: it heals you when you fall low.
    • A run that dies still pays its kills' experience, so a failed try is never wasted.
