    Out of the Dark

    Out of the Dark is step 5 of The Endless Road, given by Vesper.

    Objective

    Leave the Dark Endless alive with 3 kills.

    If you die in the Dark, you lose everything you carry. Beat three enemies and get out alive.Vesper

    Requirements

    • Turn in Onslaught first.
    • It can be worked on before level 30, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Cultist Book

    1,190 experience.
