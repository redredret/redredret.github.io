    Red Horizon

    Red Horizon is step 7 of The Endless Road, given by Vesper.

    Objective

    Reach a Red Desert region in the Endless.

    Exactly: Find 1 of: Dust Bowl, Sand Storm, Pyramids, Sand Cave, Research Station, Hellish Desert.

    Past the tower the grass gives out and the sand starts. Go and see it.Vesper

    Requirements

    • Turn in Unbroken first.
    • It can be worked on before level 35, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Adamantite Ingot ×4
    • Ipe Plank ×4

    600 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Reach any one of these in an Endless Dungeon or Dark run:
      • Dust Bowl: it opens when you clear it in the Red Desert Story
      • Sand Storm: it opens when you clear it in the Red Desert Story
      • Pyramids: it opens when you clear it in the Red Desert Story
      • Sand Cave: it opens when you clear it in the Red Desert Story
      • Research Station: it opens when you clear it in the Red Desert Story
      • Hellish Desert: it opens when you clear it in the Red Desert Story
    2. Plan a course to it on the world map before the run, or keep going until the road takes you there.
    3. Turn it in to Vesper on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • The Dust Bowl is the nearest of them. Plan a course to it on the world map before the run.
