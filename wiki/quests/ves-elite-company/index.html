    Elite Company

    Elite Company is step 2 of The Far Edge, given by Vesper.

    Objective

    Claim the Elite tier of any region's diary.

    Knowing a region and mastering it are different things. Finish one of its diaries, all the way.Vesper

    Requirements

    • Turn in Every Corner first.
    • It can be worked on before level 63, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Treasure Seal ×2
    • Gambler's Brew ×2
    • Scholar's Tea ×2

    25,670 experience.
