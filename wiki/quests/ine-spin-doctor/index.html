    Spin Doctor

    Spin Doctor is step 3 of The Drillmaster, given by Ines.

    Objective

    Land 50 T-Spins that clear lines.

    Now do fifty T-Spins. Keep going until you don't have to think about them.Ines

    Requirements

    • Turn in Four at Once first.
    • It can be worked on before level 8, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Wizard Robe
