    Set the Fangs

    Set the Fangs is step 4 of The Worm's Teeth, given by Vesper.

    Objective

    Craft the Barbed Buckler.

    Teeth out, edge in. Make one and let's see it bite.Vesper

    Requirements

    • Turn in Fangs Out first.
    • It can be worked on before level 45, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Respec Shard ×2

    1,455 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. The recipe opens when Fangs Out is turned in, the step before this one.
    2. Get Spiked Buckler ×1, the piece the recipe upgrades in place (it keeps its Forge roll and ornament): crafted from level 35 from Adamantite Buckler ×1, Broken Horn ×1 and Punchy's Skin ×1.
    3. Get Worm Fang ×20: dropped by Desert Worm (1/3 a roll, 1–2 at a time) or Wormling (1/39 a roll); in the region chests of Dust Bowl.
    4. Craft Barbed Buckler: it needs level 45.
    5. Turn it in to Vesper on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • Worm Fangs come one kill in three from the Desert Worm; the Dust Bowl region chests pay them too.
    • The Spiked Buckler you upgrade keeps its Forge roll and ornament.
