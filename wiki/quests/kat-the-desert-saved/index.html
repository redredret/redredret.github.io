    The Desert Saved

    The Desert Saved is step 7 of Across the Sand, given by Katherine.

    Objective

    Complete the Red Desert Story.

    Exactly: Finish the Red Desert Story.

    See it through to the end, like last time. I'll be here when you get back.Katherine

    Requirements

    • Turn in Shut the Gate first.
    • It can be worked on before level 36, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Riveted Plate ×3
    • Smoked Lens
    • Gold Coin ×3,000

    3,275 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. The Red Desert Story opens once the Greenshore Story is complete.
    2. Dust Bowl: 4 fights, ending with Desert Worm.
    3. Sand Storm: 3 fights, ending with Scrap Warlord.
    4. Pyramids: 4 fights, ending with Possessed Sarcophagus.
    5. Sand Cave: 4 fights, ending with Sandstone Golem.
    6. Research Station: 4 fights, ending with The Old Man.
    7. Hellish Desert: 1 fights, ending with The Unbound.
    8. Beat the last of them and the Story is complete.
    9. Turn it in to Katherine on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • Each place on the road has its own page, and each boss its attacks shown on the board.
    • Put food in your food slot before a long road: it heals you when you fall low.
    • A run that dies still pays its kills' experience, so a failed try is never wasted.
