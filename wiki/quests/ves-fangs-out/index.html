    Fangs Out

    Fangs Out is step 3 of The Worm's Teeth, given by Vesper.

    Objective

    Defeat the Desert Worm as you shoot him out.

    Finish him the moment he comes up. That's when he's soft. Do that and I'll show you how to set his fangs in a buckler.Vesper

    Requirements

    • Turn in Not a Bite first.
    • It can be worked on before level 45, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Treasure Seal ×2

    4,370 experience.

    Teaches

    • Barbed Buckler: the recipe can be made once this quest is turned in.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find Desert Worm:
      • Dust Bowl, in the Red Desert Story (fight 4 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Dust Bowl: a round boss; the warden its chest follows
    2. Know its moves: Worm Chomp and Worm Burrow (each is on its page, with a clip of the board).
    3. Beat it this way: Defeat the Desert Worm with the clear that shoots him out of his burrow.
    4. Turn it in to Vesper on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • The killing blow must be the clear that shoots him out of his burrow.
    • Bring him low first, then hold your big clear: an ordinary clear that kills him by accident does not count.
    • Keep a line ready through the bottom of your stack for when he goes under.
