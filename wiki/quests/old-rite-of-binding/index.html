    Out of Hell

    Out of Hell is the first of The Rite of Binding, given by The Old Man.

    Objective

    Carry the Rite home to Greenshore Fields.

    Exactly: Show Rite of Binding ×1.

    The rite they bound me with is still in what came through. Kill it and take the rite, then carry it all the way home to Greenshore Fields before you leave. If you don't, it burns itself up.The Old Man

    Requirements

    • Finish the Greenshore Story.
    • Reach level 55.
    • Finish the Red Desert Story.
    • It can be worked on before level 55, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Gold Coin ×3,000
    • Respec Shard

    3,790 experience.

    Quest items

    • Rite of Binding from The Unbound, carried home to Greenshore Fields in the same run
