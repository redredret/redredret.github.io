    Out of the Red Dark

    Out of the Red Dark is step 9 of The Endless Road, given by Vesper.

    Objective

    Beat a desert boss and leave the Dark alive.

    Beat a desert boss in the Dark and get out with everything you found.Vesper

    Requirements

    • Turn in Sixty Deep first.
    • It can be worked on before level 50, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Darkwood Staff

    4,660 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Start a Dark Endless Dungeon run. A death in the Dark costs gear, so wear what you can stand to lose.
    2. Defeat at least 3 enemies.
    3. One of them must be a Red Desert boss: Desert Worm, Scrap Warlord, Possessed Sarcophagus, Sandstone Golem, The Old Man or The Unbound. Plan a course into the desert on the world map before the run.
    4. Leave alive: take EXIT at a Dark checkpoint while you still stand. A run that dies does not count.
    5. Turn it in to Vesper on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • Plan a course on the world map that reaches a desert boss early, then leave at the next checkpoint.
    • Wear gear you can stand to lose.
