    Loose Ends

    Loose Ends is the first of What the Gate Kept, given by The Old Man.

    Objective

    Defeat 10 Sand Veil Cultists.

    They'll try again, so thin them out before they find another key.The Old Man

    Requirements

    • Finish the Greenshore Story.
    • Finish the Red Desert Story.
    • It can be worked on before level 50, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Gold Coin ×3,000

    4,660 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find Sand Veil Cultist:
      • Research Station, in the Red Desert Story (fights 1, 2 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Research Station: an ordinary fight, 100% of them
    2. Defeat Sand Veil Cultist 10 times.
    3. Turn it in to The Old Man on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • The Research Station Story road opens with two Sand Veil Cultists; every ordinary fight in its Endless region is one.
