    Grand Builder

    Grand Builder is step 11 of The Homestead, given by Hollis.

    Objective

    Build every level-four station.

    Exactly: Own every one of: Mines Lv 4, Trees Lv 4, Plants Lv 4.

    All three, to level four. Nobody in the valley has done that.Hollis

    Requirements

    • Turn in Deepest Roots first.
    • It can be worked on before level 55, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Adamantite Ingot ×10
    • Ipe Plank ×10
    • Nettle Fabric ×10

    18,945 experience.
