    Wake the Dead

    Wake the Dead is step 3 of Across the Sand, given by Katherine.

    Objective

    Defeat the Possessed Sarcophagus.

    Exactly: Defeat Possessed Sarcophagus 1 times, all told.

    Somebody broke into the Pyramids, and now something in there won't stay put. Go and put it back.Katherine

    Requirements

    • Turn in Dock His Pay first.
    • It can be worked on before level 32, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Adamantite Buckler

    935 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find Possessed Sarcophagus:
      • Pyramids, in the Red Desert Story (fight 4 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Pyramids: a round boss; the warden its chest follows
    2. Defeat Possessed Sarcophagus once.
    3. Turn it in to Katherine on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • Clear the row the Possessed Sarcophagus's stone sits in and your next hit does double.
    • When he raises a Mummy, it takes your next hit. Break it with a small clear and save the big one.
    • Fill the holes his spirits burst in your stack. He is weak to spins.
