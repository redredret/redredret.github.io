    Put Them Out

    Put Them Out is the first of Sun and Sand, given by Katherine.

    Objective

    Clear 10 Sun Brands before they flare.

    The Sand Veil brand your blocks with sun-fire. Clear a branded row before it flares and the brand goes out. Put out ten.Katherine

    Requirements

    • Finish the Greenshore Story.
    • Reach level 48.
    • Defeat the Lesser Cult Leader 3 times.
    • It can be worked on before level 48, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Gold Coin ×2,000

    3,855 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find Sand Veil Cultist:
      • Research Station, in the Red Desert Story (fights 1, 2 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Research Station: an ordinary fight, 100% of them
    2. ▶Wait for Sand Veil Cultist's Sun Brands. Three sun-bolts brand three of your blocks. After 3 seconds, each brand still there flares, burns away its block and the one beside it, and hits you. Clear their rows first to put them out.
    3. Do it 10 times in all: Clear 10 Sun Brands before they flare. Every answer counts, in any mode, over as many fights as it takes.
    4. Turn it in to Katherine on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • A Sand Veil Cultist's Sun Brands mark three of your blocks. Clear a branded block's row inside three seconds and its brand goes out.
    • Each brand put out counts, so one volley can count up to three.
