    Fill the Gap

    Fill the Gap is step 2 of Breaking Stone, given by Vesper.

    Objective

    T-Spin into the Golem's T-Gap.

    With a T coming, he splits the ground and leaves a T-shaped hole. Spin the T into it. Three times.Vesper

    Requirements

    • Turn in Fault Lines first.
    • It can be worked on before level 45, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Gold Coin ×2,000
    • Respec Shard

    2,915 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find Sandstone Golem:
      • Sand Cave, in the Red Desert Story (fight 4 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Sand Cave: a round boss; the warden its chest follows
    2. ▶Wait for Sandstone Golem's T-Gap. Pushes up garbage with a T-shaped slot when your stack is low and a T is coming. T-spin into it to stun him and hit twice as hard. If your stack is too tall, he cracks another block instead.
    3. Do it 3 times in all: T-Spin into the Sandstone Golem's T-Gap 3 times. Every answer counts, in any mode, over as many fights as it takes.
    4. Turn it in to Vesper on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • He makes the T-Gap only when your stack is low and a T is coming. Too tall, and he cracks a block instead.
    • Keep your stack low and watch the NEXT queue for the T. Spin it into the slot.
