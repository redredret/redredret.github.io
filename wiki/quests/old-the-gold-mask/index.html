    The Gold Mask

    The Gold Mask is step 2 of What the Gate Kept, given by The Old Man.

    Objective

    Defeat the Lesser Cult Leader 3 times.

    The one in the gold mask held my chain for years. I'd like him to remember your face.The Old Man

    Requirements

    • Turn in Loose Ends first.
    • It can be worked on before level 50, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Gold Coin ×3,000
    • Respec Shard

    4,660 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find Lesser Cult Leader:
      • Research Station, in the Red Desert Story (fight 3 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Research Station: a round boss; the warden its chest follows
    2. Defeat Lesser Cult Leader 3 times.
    3. Turn it in to The Old Man on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • The Lesser Cult Leader is the third fight of the Research Station road, and its region's round boss in the Endless Dungeon.
    • His Sun Shield takes most of every hit: break it with a spin or a double, then hit hard.
