    Champion Hunter

    Champion Hunter is step 3 of The Endless Road, given by Vesper.

    Objective

    Defeat 5 champions.

    Some enemies out there are champions. They're tougher and carry extra loot. Beat five.Vesper

    Requirements

    • Turn in Holding the Line first.
    • It can be worked on before level 20, and is turned in at it.
