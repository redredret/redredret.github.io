    Grave Goods

    Grave Goods is the first of The Ancient Mask, given by Brann.

    Objective

    Defeat Mummies until one drops its prize.

    Exactly: Show Desert Orb ×1.

    Something seems up with the mummies in the desert. They seem to be protecting something. Get it.Brann

    Requirements

    • Finish the Greenshore Story.
    • Reach level 55.
    • Finish the Red Desert Story.
    • It can be worked on before level 55, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Gold Coin ×2,500

    11,365 experience.

    Quest items

    • Desert Orb from Mummy, 1/10 a kill while this quest wants it

    Walkthrough

    1. Find Mummy:
      • Pyramids, in the Red Desert Story (fights 2, 3 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Pyramids: an ordinary fight, 50% of them
    2. Defeat Mummy until Desert Orb drops: 1/10 a kill, and only while this step is open. Every mode counts.
    3. Show it to Brann: turning the step in leaves it with you.

    Tips

    • The Desert Orb drops only while this step (or the next) is open and you hold none, and at most one a run.
    • It is one kill in ten, so expect about ten Mummies. The Pyramids Story road has two; an Endless run through the Pyramids deals more.
    • Keep it: the next step charges it.
