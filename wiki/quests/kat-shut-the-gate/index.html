    Shut the Gate

    Shut the Gate is step 6 of Across the Sand, given by Katherine.

    Objective

    Defeat the Unbound.

    Exactly: Defeat The Unbound 1 times, all told.

    The whole sky's gone red. Whatever came through that gate, send it back.Katherine

    Requirements

    • Turn in The Prisoner first.
    • It can be worked on before level 36, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Treasure Seal ×2
    • Ironbark Tonic ×2
    • Gold Coin ×1,500

    2,620 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find The Unbound:
      • Hellish Desert, in the Red Desert Story (fight 1 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Hellish Desert: a round boss; the warden its chest follows
    2. Defeat The Unbound once.
    3. Turn it in to Katherine on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • The Unbound changes twice as he weakens. Answer each of his moves and your next hit lands double.
    • Demand: make three clears in a row inside 14 seconds. Missed, a row of hell-iron rises under your stack.
    • Ward Sigil: lock the piece it outlines into it inside 8 seconds. Brand of Sin: clear both branded rows with one piece.
    • Hell's Bag rewrites your next three pieces: HOLD is the way round it. He is weak to spins.
