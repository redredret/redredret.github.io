    Not a Bite

    Not a Bite is step 2 of The Worm's Teeth, given by Vesper.

    Objective

    Defeat the Desert Worm with no block eaten.

    While he's underground, he eats a block of your tower every few seconds. Beat him without losing a single block to his burrow.Vesper

    Requirements

    • Turn in Under the Sand first.
    • It can be worked on before level 45, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Ironbark Tonic ×2

    4,370 experience.
