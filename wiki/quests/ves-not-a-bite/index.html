    Not a Bite

    Not a Bite is step 2 of The Worm's Teeth, given by Vesper.

    Objective

    Defeat the Desert Worm with no block eaten.

    While he's underground, he eats a block of your tower every few seconds. Beat him without losing a single block to his burrow.Vesper

    Requirements

    • Turn in Under the Sand first.
    • It can be worked on before level 45, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Ironbark Tonic ×2

    4,370 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find Desert Worm:
      • Dust Bowl, in the Red Desert Story (fight 4 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Dust Bowl: a round boss; the warden its chest follows
    2. Know its moves: Worm Chomp and Worm Burrow (each is on its page, with a clip of the board).
    3. Beat it this way: Defeat the Desert Worm without his burrow eating a block.
    4. Turn it in to Vesper on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • Only his burrow counts against you: his chomp's bites do not.
    • Shoot him out of his burrow the moment he goes under: a line cleared through his dirt ends it, before he eats a block.
    • If a block goes, you need another fight, so keep trying on the next Worm.
