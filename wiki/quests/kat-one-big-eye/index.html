    One Big Eye

    One Big Eye is step 2 of Trouble in Greenshore, given by Katherine.

    Objective

    Defeat the Cyclops.

    A big one-eyed brute jumped me out there. Go find him.Katherine

    Requirements

    • Turn in Into Greenshore first.
    • It can be worked on before level 3, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Iron Helmet

    115 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find Cyclops:
      • Greenshore, in the Greenshore Story (fight 4 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Greenshore Fields: a round boss; the warden its chest follows
    2. Defeat Cyclops once.
    3. Turn it in to Katherine on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • The Cyclops is weak to spins: a T-spin hits it 10% harder.
    • Its punch knocks two or three of your top blocks loose and drops them in one clump. Fill in around the clump before you build over it.
    • Its fireball lights two of your top blocks for a second. A piece set on a burning block burns away, so set it somewhere else.
