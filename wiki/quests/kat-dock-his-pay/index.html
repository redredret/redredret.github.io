    Dock His Pay

    Dock His Pay is step 2 of Across the Sand, given by Katherine.

    Objective

    Defeat the Scrap Warlord.

    Exactly: Defeat Scrap Warlord 1 times, all told.

    Raiders are robbing every cart out of the desert. Their boss pays them in scrap. Stop the payments.Katherine

    Requirements

    • Turn in Something Under the Sand first.
    • It can be worked on before level 26, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Adamantite Ingot ×5
    • Ipe Plank ×5

    575 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find Scrap Warlord:
      • Sand Storm, in the Red Desert Story (fight 3 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Sand Storm: a round boss; the warden its chest follows
    2. Defeat Scrap Warlord once.
    3. Turn it in to Katherine on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • Clear a line while the Scrap Warlord's coin is in the air and his crew dies for good.
    • Any hit stops his Dust Devil, so keep a clear ready.
    • He is weak to combos.
