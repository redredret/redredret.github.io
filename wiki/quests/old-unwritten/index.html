    Unwritten

    Unwritten is step 2 of The Rite of Binding, given by The Old Man.

    Objective

    Bring the Old Man the Rite of Binding.

    Exactly: Hand over Rite of Binding ×1.

    Give it here. Bound into a book with the cult's eye and the lotus, it will answer to whoever carries it, not to them.The Old Man

    Requirements

    • Turn in Out of Hell first.
    • It can be worked on before level 55, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Gold Coin ×5,000

    3,790 experience.

    Teaches

    • Unbound Focus: the recipe can be made once this quest is turned in.

    Quest items

    • Rite of Binding from The Unbound, carried home to Greenshore Fields in the same run
