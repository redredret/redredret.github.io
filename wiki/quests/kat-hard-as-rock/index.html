    Hard as Rock

    Hard as Rock is step 4 of Across the Sand, given by Katherine.

    Objective

    Defeat the Sandstone Golem.

    Exactly: Defeat Sandstone Golem 1 times, all told.

    There's a cave under the tombs, and the cave has a guard. A very big one.Katherine

    Requirements

    • Turn in Wake the Dead first.
    • It can be worked on before level 32, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Adamantite Armor

    935 experience.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find Sandstone Golem:
      • Sand Cave, in the Red Desert Story (fight 4 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Sand Cave: a round boss; the warden its chest follows
    2. Defeat Sandstone Golem once.
    3. Turn it in to Katherine on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • The Sandstone Golem uses four attacks in turn, and each wants its own answer.
    • Fault Line: dig down and clear the cracked row to stun him and hit twice as hard.
    • Dust Blast hides your NEXT pieces for 8 seconds, but HOLD still shows: keep a good piece in it.
    • Heavy Hands makes your pieces fall fast for 6 seconds. Keep each placement simple.
    • T-Gap: with a T coming and your stack low, he leaves a T-shaped slot. T-spin into it. He is weak to Triples and Quads.
