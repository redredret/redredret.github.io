    Deep Harvest

    Deep Harvest is step 7 of The Homestead, given by Hollis.

    Objective

    Reach level 15 in a single Farm run.

    Exactly: Reach level 15 in one Farm run.

    The longer a Farm run lasts, the better it pays. Get one to level 15.Hollis

    Requirements

    • Turn in A Working Farm first.
    • It can be worked on before level 20, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Mithril Ingot ×8
    • Maple Plank ×8
    • Cotton Fabric ×8

    725 experience.
