    Spoils of the Road

    Spoils of the Road is the first of The Endless Road, given by Vesper.

    Objective

    Open a chest in the Endless or its Dark.

    After the Story, the road keeps going in Endless and Dark. There are chests out there. Open one.Vesper

    Requirements

    • It can be worked on before level 12, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards
