    Souls for the Orb

    Souls for the Orb is step 2 of The Ancient Mask, given by Brann.

    Objective

    Defeat 5 desert bosses in a run with the Orb.

    That's an old artifact, and it needs charging in the desert. It loses its power over time, so it has to be done in one trip. Put it in your quest slot and see if it takes the souls of every desert boss.Brann

    Requirements

    • Turn in Grave Goods first.
    • It can be worked on before level 55, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Gold Coin ×3,000
    • Respec Shard

    18,945 experience.

    Quest items

    • Desert Orb from Mummy, 1/10 a kill while this quest wants it

    Walkthrough

    1. Put Desert Orb in the QUEST slot of your boosts before the run.
    2. Start an Endless Dungeon or Dark Endless Dungeon run, alone or in a party.
    3. In that one run, defeat every one of these, in any order:
      • Desert Worm: Dust Bowl
      • Scrap Warlord: Sand Storm
      • Possessed Sarcophagus: Pyramids
      • Sandstone Golem: Sand Cave
      • Lesser Cult Leader: Research Station
    4. It glows as it charges and fills on the last of them. The charge is counted from the run's kills when the run is over, and a run that misses one starts again from none.
    5. Turn it in to Brann on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • Plan a course through all five desert regions on the world map before you start. It is a long run, so bring food.
    • The orb must be in the QUEST slot when the run starts: the slot is shut while a run is open.
    • The Unbound does not count. A run that ends short of the five loses the charge but never the orb, even a Dark death.
    • In a party, everyone carrying an orb is charged by the party's kills.
