    Souls for the Orb

    Souls for the Orb is step 2 of The Ancient Mask, given by Brann.

    Objective

    Defeat 5 desert bosses in a run with the Orb.

    That's an old artifact, and it needs charging in the desert. It loses its power over time, so it has to be done in one trip. Put it in your quest slot and see if it takes the souls of every desert boss.Brann

    Requirements

    • Turn in Grave Goods first.
    • It can be worked on before level 55, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Gold Coin ×3,000
    • Respec Shard

    18,945 experience.

    Quest items

    • Desert Orb from Mummy, 1/10 a kill while this quest wants it
