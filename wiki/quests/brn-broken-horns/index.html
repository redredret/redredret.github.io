    Broken Horns

    Broken Horns is step 3 of The Warlord's Iron, given by Brann.

    Objective

    Defeat the Warlord, crew dead, no Dust Devil.

    Now both at once. Kill his crew, keep the Devil off your tower, and bring him down. Then I'll show you how to melt his helm onto an Orichalcum one.Brann

    Requirements

    • Turn in Still Air first.
    • It can be worked on before level 50, and is turned in at it.

    Rewards

    • Gold Coin ×3,000

    6,985 experience.

    Teaches

    • Orichalcum Warhelm: the recipe can be made once this quest is turned in.

    Walkthrough

    1. Find Scrap Warlord:
      • Sand Storm, in the Red Desert Story (fight 3 of the road)
      • the Endless Dungeon's Sand Storm: a round boss; the warden its chest follows
    2. Know its moves: Pay The Crew and Dust Devil (each is on its page, with a clip of the board).
    3. Beat it this way: Defeat the Scrap Warlord with his crew dead and no Dust Devil ripping your tower.
    4. Turn it in to Brann on the QUESTS screen.

    Tips

    • His crew must be dead when he falls, and no Dust Devil may rip a block all fight.
    • Keep a clear ready at all times: one for the coin, one for the Devil.
    • If the Devil rips a block, the fight no longer counts. Finish it and try the next Warlord.
