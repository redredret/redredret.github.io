    Blood Pact

    Blood Pact is a pact: an omen drawn after every 3rd chest in a run with omens on.

    What it changes

    • Enemy hits +100%
    • Your damage +100%
    • Drop tables rolled +100% as often

    A run with omens on pays 20% more experience. The Endless Dungeon guide explains drawing them.
